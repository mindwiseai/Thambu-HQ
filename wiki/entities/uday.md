## Overview
Uday runs [[racing-point-esports]] in Hyderabad. Within [[ierl]], he is the technical lead for event execution and the creator of VMS (Vehicle Management Software / event management software) that will power the league.

## VMS Software
Uday developed VMS (name used in session: "Uday's software" / "VMS software"), a purpose-built platform for sim racing event management:
- Event management
- Leaderboards
- Championships
- Designed specifically for the Indian market

As of June 2026, VMS is close to ready. Once live, it will handle all IERL event operations and reduce operational load for all partner centres.

Uday has iterated on VMS after running multiple hot lap events and identified gaps in the market — the current version addresses those.

## IERL responsibilities
- Leads hot lap event format and technical execution on the ground
- Has run hot lap events multiple times — experienced operator
- VMS will power all future events: hot laps, quarterly championships, etc.
