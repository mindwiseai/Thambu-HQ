# RaceSims Chennai Flagship Centre — Multi-Session Planning

Strategic planning conversation (May 17, 2026) where [[thambu]] developed the full concept, financial model, competition architecture, software platform, India saturation roadmap, and revenue stack for a [[racesims-chennai-flagship|RaceSims Experience Centre]] in Chennai under [[racesims-company|RaceSims Solutions Pvt Ltd]] as a direct operation.

## Summary

The conversation evolved from "should we open a 4-6 seat sim centre in Nungambakkam?" into a complete strategic platform plan covering: (1) the [[racesims-chennai-flagship|Chennai flagship venue]] economics, (2) the [[racesims-drive-train-compete|Drive · Train · Compete positioning framework]], (3) a 4-layer [[racesims-championship-architecture|Championship architecture]] using LMU + iRacing + AC Rally + Richard Burns Rally as the locked platform stack, (4) [[racesims-the-ladder|the Ladder]] sim-to-reality pathway anchored on [[arka-motorsports|ARKA Rally Drive]] and Polo Cup Follow Car prizes, (5) a custom [[racesims-vms-build|VMS software platform]] to be built in-house instead of paying ₹30K/month to simracing.co.uk, (6) the [[racesims-india-saturation-plan|India saturation roadmap]] to 43 centres in 5 years, and (7) a deep dive into [[racesims-revenue-stack|six additional revenue streams]] (AMC, Junior Motorsport Academy, Merch, Coaching & Engineering Academy, Karting Academy, College Curriculum).

## Key facts

- **Total flagship investment locked: ₹66 lakhs** (₹30.18L hardware + ₹15.45L lean industrial infrastructure + ₹1.60L software + ₹3L café + ₹15.80L working capital) — VMS build adds ₹2L taking total to ₹67–68 lakhs depending on whether internal Driver Card system is built immediately or deferred
- **Monthly opex locked at ₹4.97L** including ₹75K director remuneration to [[thambu]] from Day 1 (no deferral)
- **Floor space required: 1,800–2,000 sqft built-up** in Anna Nagar / Kilpauk / Alwarpet at ₹1.40L target rent
- **Year 1 EBITDA projection: ₹32 lakhs.** Full payback: ~17 months. Year 2 EBITDA: ₹87L
- **Racing seat cost correction: ₹30K per seat** (was ₹12K in earlier drafts) — total hardware capex moves from ₹29.10L → ₹30.18L
- **6 seats locked from Day 1** (not 4-seat phased launch) — premium positioning requires the visual statement of 6 rigs
- **Café stays as full build** (₹3L capex, ₹1.5L/month net contribution at maturity) — vending alternative rejected as value-destroying
- **VMS to be built in-house by Claude with junior dev support** — ₹2L build cost vs. ₹3.6L/year ongoing SaaS fees from simracing.co.uk; pays back in 7 months at Chennai alone, dramatically better at network scale
- **Software stack locked**: Le Mans Ultimate + iRacing (Circuit), AC Rally + Richard Burns Rally (Rally). ACC and Assetto Corsa explicitly excluded.
- **Insurance ₹8K/month confirmed** as 3-policy bundle (equipment ₹3.5K, public liability ₹2.5K, business interruption ₹2K)
- **5-year India saturation reaches 43 centres** (7 Mega Flagships in Tier 1 + 36 Boutiques across 8 Tier 1 + 18 Tier 2 cities). Network revenue ₹105 cr, RaceSims direct revenue ₹83 cr, EBITDA ₹33 cr, valuation ₹1,000–1,300 cr. With the 6 adjacencies added: revenue ₹99 cr, EBITDA ₹41 cr, valuation ₹1,250–1,650 cr.
- **Global hyper-scale scenario** (with VC capital): 70+ centres across 25 countries in 3-5 years, ₹6,000–12,000 cr valuation, $700M–$1.4B unicorn outcome. India-first strongly preferred for execution probability and founder ownership retention.

## Decisions locked

1. **Chennai flagship is direct-operated** under [[racesims-company|RaceSims Solutions Pvt Ltd]] — no new entity, no franchisee
2. **Positioning is [[racesims-drive-train-compete|Drive · Train · Compete]]** — three-word framework that all marketing flows from
3. **Championship architecture is 4-layer**: Always-On + 5-Round Quarterly Season + Quarterly Champion + Grand Final (Dec)
4. **Two parallel championships** run year-round: Circuit (LMU + iRacing) and Rally (AC Rally + Richard Burns Rally)
5. **The Ladder**: Win Initiation Programme + Win a Live Race Round + Win the Annual Grand Final = Real-Drive Prize (Polo Cup Follow Car ₹1.5L or ARKA Rally Drive ₹1L)
6. **VMS built in-house** — drops the ₹30K/month simracing.co.uk subscription decision, replaces with custom Node.js/PostgreSQL/Next.js platform
7. **VMS architected as multi-tenant SaaS-ready from Day 1** so it can scale to franchise network and external SaaS customers without rebuild
8. **Lean industrial infrastructure aesthetic** — exposed beams, polished concrete, partition walls — positioning fit + ₹2.4L capex saving
9. **Café full build retained** — best ROI in the entire investment (2-month payback, ₹15L+ annual net contribution)
10. **5-year India saturation plan is the base strategy** (Path B in the conversation framing); global option deferred to Year 4+
