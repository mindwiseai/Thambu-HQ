# Bambu Lab X1 Carbon

CoreXY enclosed-chamber FDM 3D printer selected by [[racesims-company|RaceSims]] for [[in-house-3d-printing]] accessories manufacturing. Purchased as the X1C Combo (printer + AMS + hardened nozzles + filament dryer).

## Key specs

- **Build volume:** 256 x 256 x 256 mm
- **Auto-calibration:** LIDAR-based
- **Multi-colour:** AMS 4-colour system — enables branded parts without post-processing
- **Enclosure:** fully enclosed heated chamber, required for reliable ASA/ABS/PA-CF printing in Indian summer conditions

## Indian service

Serviced locally via Dimensionex, 3Ding, and Wol3D.

## Decision context

Selected over Prusa MK4 (no enclosure) and Creality K1 family (shorter service reach in South India). Full rationale in [[2026-04-23-3d-printing-capex-decision]].
