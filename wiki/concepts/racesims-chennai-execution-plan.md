# RaceSims · Chennai — Execution Plan (Variant C)

The full build-and-launch plan for the chosen [[racesims-chennai-premium-baseline|Variant C]] centre: **8 rigs (7 race/rally + 1 three-DOF motion), 2 staff, sim-only baseline**, run under [[racesims-company|Racesims Solutions Pvt Ltd]]. Created 2026-05-29. Target: **decision → public open in ~16 weeks**, profitable from ~month 3, payback ~13 months.

## Layout (final — see `racesims-chennai-floorplan.png` + `racesims-chennai-3d.html`)
**L-shape:** 4 rigs along the back wall + 3 along the right wall + the **motion rig as a hero island** (centre-left, visible on entry, 3 m clearance + spot lighting — the Instagram magnet). **Entrance** on the lift-lobby wall (per markup), with a combined **reception + coffee counter** just inside it on the right. **Two washroom doors** on the front-left wall are kept clear (no fixtures in that zone). **Glass-partitioned training / academy room** (~11.5 m², 6–8 seats) in the back-left corner — enables the Phase-2 [[racesims-motorsport-academy|Academy]] without moving a rig. Lounge/spectator strip centre, 65″ leaderboard on the right wall. Floor-ducted cabling, acoustic panels behind the rig rows, ACs high on end walls. Total feature footprint ~55% of the 1,142 sq ft hall. Editorial: cream base + RS Red accent, Montserrat headings, JetBrains Mono leaderboard.

## Budget reconciliation
> [!contradiction]
> The financial model's **₹3,05,500** monthly OpEx is *core fixed lights-on only*. The execution plan's fuller itemisation adds sim subscriptions (~₹9k), single-venue VMS cloud (~₹3–5k), insurance (~₹7.5k), payment gateway (~2% of revenue) and Y1 marketing (~₹25k) → realistic all-in **~₹3.6–3.9L/mo**. Break-even still ~12–13% on the larger figure. **Fold these lines into the Excel before final sign-off.**

Capital ₹68.3L net (sunk ₹59.0L). Hold **~₹17L runway** separate (covers worst-case ₹7.6L drawdown + motion-rig repair contingency). Ring-fence personal house rent.

## KPIs & guardrails
Weekly: rig-hrs sold, motion utilisation, revenue, new Driver Cards, repeat %. **Month-4 checkpoint:** occupancy >10% & climbing, drawdown <₹10L, ≥2 corporate bookings, motion >30% booked — fail any two → mitigate. Layer [[racesims-motorsport-academy|academy]] at >15% sustained; café at >20%; 2nd floor (typical-floor building, 1st–4th available) when weekday peak >60% for 3 months → feeds [[racesims-india-saturation-plan]].
