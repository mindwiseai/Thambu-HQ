About the league

A national hot-lap series, settled by the clock.

The Indian Esports Racing League is a monthly time-trial championship run on Assetto Corsa across the RaceSims partner network. Each round, every partner runs the same car around the same circuit. Drivers post their fastest lap, the times are aggregated into a single national board, and championship points are scored across the season.

Format

Each round opens on the first of the month and closes at month end. Drivers may post times at any IERL partner during the window. Only the fastest single valid lap counts toward the round result. There is no qualifying, no race, no penalties — just the lap.

Scoring

Points are awarded by finishing position in each round on an F1-style scale: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1. Each driver's worst two rounds across the season are dropped. Tied totals are settled by countback — most wins first, then most second places, and so on.

Eligibility

Open to any driver who sets a lap on a registered IERL partner rig during the round window. Laps flagged invalid by the timing system (cut track, off-track, technical infraction) are excluded automatically.

Data

The leaderboard pulls from public timing pages — RSR Live Timing, AC Server Manager, and partner-hosted result boards — refreshed hourly. If you spot a missing or incorrect time, raise it with your partner; corrections flow back from source pages on the next refresh cycle.
