02 · What is RaceSims?

India's integrated motorsport development platform.

Indian motorsport is in a moment most of us have waited a long time for. F1 viewership is growing every year, the INRC is delivering season after season of close racing, and teams like ARKA Motorsports have spent four decades building championship pedigree. The audience is there. The talent is there. What's been narrow is the runway in between — the path from loving racing to racing.

That's the gap RaceSims is built to fill. A place where someone who loves racing can develop the craft, compete in real championships, and earn a path toward real-world seats through partners like ARKA and Polo Cup. We're not replacing the motorsport ecosystem. We're adding a new on-ramp to it.

01
The equipment
Direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, load-cell pedals — the same technology behind F1 driver simulators — has finally become affordable enough to deploy commercially in India.
02
The pedigree
Founded by a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports (130+ national championship titles). Every curriculum, every coaching session, every centre standard is shaped by motorsport people. The Ladder pathway to real-world seats is real.
03
The integration
Direct distributor in India for both Conspit and VNM. We get the gear at trade pricing — a ₹65L centre built for ₹39L. And we run the full stack: centres + hardware + championship + curriculum + sim-to-real pathway.

The dream is common. What's been missing is a place to develop it.

There's a particular kind of dream that nearly every Indian teenager has held at some point — racing driver. F1 broadcasts on Sundays, INRC at Coimbatore, MotoGP reruns, Drive to Survive on Netflix. The audience for motorsport in India is in the millions and growing 25%+ a year. The dream is common. We are building the place to develop it.
