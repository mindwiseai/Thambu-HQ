Sim racing in India is where karting was in 2010.

Why now

  • F1 viewership in India crossed 50M last season, growing 25%+ YoY
  • 425M+ gamers in India; 10M+ play racing games regularly
  • Drive to Survive among Netflix India's most-watched non-fiction
  • Direct-drive hardware now affordable; motion platforms credible sub-₹10L
  • Esports recognised by Govt of India (2022) — competitive sim racing has a path

Why Chennai

  • MMRT — India's premier race circuit is here
  • 60-year motorsport heritage — Tamil Nadu's racing ecosystem
  • MRF Motorsport · ARKA Coimbatore axis · INRC rounds locally
  • Significant HNI & supercar owner base · MMRT track-day community
  • Indian motorsport heroes (Narain Karthikeyan, Ehsan Wazir et al) come from here

Why us

  • India distributor for Conspit & VNM — premium rigs at trade pricing
  • ARKA Motorsports lineage — 130+ championship titles, real racing credibility
  • Existing dealer network across 4 cities running our Hot Lap pool
  • National Sim Racing Championship announced for 2027
  • Operator + distributor + championship organiser — integrated stack
