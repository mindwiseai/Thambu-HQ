# Chennai — Fit-Out Real-World Sourcing & Line-by-Line Optimisation

## Summary
[[thambu]] pushed back on the carried-forward ₹16.10L fit-out estimate ("where are these numbers from?"). It was never a real quote — a planning figure repeated across drafts. We re-grounded all 17 lines bottom-up from **actual 2026 India listings and rate-cards** (IndiaMART, Philips, Moglix, Chennai contractors) plus top-down benchmarks (Cushman & Wakefield, JLL), then walked every line with [[thambu]] to optimise. Result: fit-out **₹16.10L → ₹14.20L launch**, fully sourced and *more complete* than before. The [[racesims-chennai-fitout]] page holds the living schedule; the landed report was rebuilt on it. See [[2026-05-30-chennai-landed-cost-bom]] for the equipment side.

## Key facts
- **The old ₹16.1L was a *lean* number, not premium.** ₹16.1L ÷ 1,288 sqft = ₹1,240/sqft = bottom-of-mid-range. Real Indian benchmarks: mid ₹1,200–1,800/sqft, **premium ₹1,800–2,700/sqft** (C&W/JLL). Honest premium cost (sourced MID) = **₹21.3L** (₹1,650/sqft).
- **Optimised to ₹14.2L** via value-engineering + phasing + sponsor-shift, *without* gutting the premium surfaces — a ₹7.1L cut from the honest premium.
- **The old estimate also under-counted:** it buried the **UPS** (real electrical+UPS ₹2.25L vs ₹0.90L) and **omitted WiFi entirely**. The new ₹14.2L is lower *and* complete.
- **Carpet tiles, not hard floor** — [[thambu]]'s call: carpet the whole 1,100 sqft (rest is bathrooms), rigs sit on top. Saved ~₹1L vs SPC. Use commercial/contract-grade *tiles* (replaceable).
- **Networking re-spec'd (2026):** 24-port PoE switch was over-specced (PCs don't take PoE; only APs/cameras do, cameras run off NVR's own PoE). Right-sized to 16-port managed Gigabit + WiFi-6 AP + ER605 dual-WAN. Latest-spec ≠ newest: Gigabit LAN (not 2.5/10G — racing is latency-bound), WiFi 6 (not 6E/7), Cat6 (not 6A).

## Decisions (locked with [[thambu]], 2026-05-30)
- Carpet tiles (1,100 sqft) · black-framed glass partition (cheaper *and* fits the industrial look) · 4-camera CCTV (not 6) · attendance-only biometric (main door is a **shutter** — no door lock) · 65″ QLED leaderboard (budget-mid, ₹0 if Samsung sponsors) · tablet POS.
- **Skipped at launch:** acoustic panels (hear the room first) + ambient audio → Phase 2.
- Furniture re-scoped: one counter-height **ledge + premium bar stools** (spectator/coffee perch) + training-room chairs — kept at ₹1.00L for premium chairs.
- AC stays **8 T** (4×2 T 5-star) — do NOT undersize to 6 T (8 PCs + Chennai heat).
- UPS covers **PCs only**; motion rig drops on power loss. UPS sits **outdoors on a grill → needs IP-rated weatherproof enclosure** + heat-derating watch (lithium vs VRLA).

## Capital impact

CoC) · 5-yr multiple **~3.1×**. EBITDA ₹3.57cr / PAT ₹2.11cr / payback ~2.0 yr hold (revenue/opex unchanged).
