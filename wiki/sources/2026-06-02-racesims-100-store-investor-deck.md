# RaceSims — 100-Store All-Owned Network Plan & Investor Deck Session

Session where the multi-centre investor pitch was rebuilt around a 100-store, all-owned-and-operated model using the locked [[racesims-chennai-flagship|Chennai template]] as the single replication unit. Championship and training revenue were stress-tested against European/global market data and rebalanced.

## Summary

Strategic pivot from the prior [[racesims-india-saturation-plan]] (43 centres: 12 direct + 31 franchise) to 100 all-owned centres. Single format: Chennai-spec (2,000 sq ft, 8 rigs, ₹95L Y1, ₹2.62 cr Y5). Y5 revenue ₹150 cr, EBITDA ₹52 cr. Series A ask ₹25-30 cr. Investor brief (3-question format) archived — multi-centre rollout deck is the sole active pitch.

## Key facts

- **Template unit:** [[racesims-chennai-flagship]] v3.2 — 2,000 sq ft · 8 rigs (7 Rally + 1 Motion) · ₹95L Y1 cash · ₹4.34L monthly opex · ₹2.62 cr Y5 revenue · 68% Y5 EBITDA margin · 25-month payback · 5.4× capital multiple
- **Rollout:** Y1=1 → Y2=8 → Y3=25 → Y4=60 → Y5=100 centres · all owned and operated
- **No franchise model:** every centre is direct-owned · 100% revenue accrues to RaceSims
- **Y5 revenue:** ₹150 cr (centre cohorts ₹121 cr + adjacencies ₹29 cr)
- **Y5 EBITDA:** ~₹52 cr (~35% blended margin)
- **Valuation:** ₹1,500-2,000 cr Y5 · ₹3,000-4,000 cr at maturity (Y8, all centres mature)
- **Capital:** Series A ₹25-30 cr + Series B ₹50-60 cr at Y3 = ₹75-90 cr total external
- **Series B proof points:** 25 operating centres + Championship Season 2 + ₹22 cr revenue run-rate
- **Championship:** reframed as brand engine at break-even (₹2.5 cr ≈ cost-neutral) — not a profit line
- **Gateway framing locked:** "RaceSims is India's gateway to motorsport" — replaces "integrated motorsport development platform" (Krishna Selvam comment)
- **4 Lines of Business (all RaceSims-branded):** RaceSims Experience Centres · RaceSims Hardware Distribution · RaceSims Motorsport Academy · RaceSims Championships (plural — Racing + Rally)
- **Investor brief archived:** `racesims-investor-brief-three-questions.*` moved to `marketing/decks/archive/`

## Research findings (European/global market)

- **F1 Arcade** (best comparable): £31.9m FY24 revenue (UK £19.8m + US £12.1m) across ~5-6 venues · still EBITDA-negative at scale · revenue split ~50% F&B / 50% racing · zero meaningful league revenue
- **Championship economics:** Global flagship leagues (F1 Esports $750K prize, FIA GT $250K) are **subsidised cost centres funded by F1/manufacturers** — not independent profit lines. No independent operator makes real money on leagues.
- **Training market:** High-margin but bespoke cottage industry globally (£150/hr in UK, $20-100/hr online). Doesn't scale into crores at HQ level.
- **Hardware is the real adjacency:** VRH already proves demand for ₹20L motion rigs in India. 100 owned showrooms = genuine ₹25 cr+ channel. Under-weighted previously, now correctly sized.
- **India esports:** ~$240M market (2026), 95% mobile gaming — sim racing is rounding error within it. Sponsorship not yet a real line.

## Decisions

- Franchise model dropped entirely — all 100 centres owned
- Mega/Boutique format split dropped — single Chennai template across all 100
- Championship revenue modelled at break-even (brand engine, not profit engine)
- Three-question investor brief archived — multi-centre rollout deck is the sole pitch artefact
- Rate card aligned: ₹750/30min · ₹1,250/1hr · Pass 8 ₹8K · Pass 16 ₹14K · Race Academy ₹20K · Rally School ₹25K
