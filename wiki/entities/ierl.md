## Overview
Indian Esports Racing League (IERL) is a sim racing league operated by [[thambu]] under the RaceSims network. It is the first structured, multi-centre esports racing league in India designed to create a sim-to-reality pathway where top drivers can eventually compete in real motorsport events.

**Website:** indianesportsracingleague.com (coming soon)
**Social handles:** secured

## Sim-to-reality vision
The defining mission of IERL: drivers progress from hot lap events → quarterly championships → the best driver earns a real race car experience. Real-world assets supporting this:
- [[arka-motorsports]]: 4 rally and race cars committed to the program
- Coimbatore test track (lower cost, dedicated)
- Coimbatore circuit (higher cost, formal)
- [[buzzing-hornet-motorsports]]: VW Polo Cup car track experiences in Chennai

## League format (as of July 2026)
- **Platform:** Assetto Corsa
- **Classes:** Amateur and Pro, each with their own leaderboard. Combined prize pool.
- **Amateur class:** ABS allowed, TC allowed
- **Pro class:** ABS allowed, TC disabled
- **Both classes:** Real Penalty track limits, fixed setup
- **Track & car combo:** Chosen monthly by the league ([[uday]] decides); announced before round start
- **Prizes:** Amazon Gift Vouchers

## Prize pool formula
Fixed pool: **₹1,00,000 every month**
Formula: ₹1,00,000 ÷ total rigs = per-rig fee

| Round | Rigs | Per-rig fee |
|-------|------|-------------|
| July 2026 (launch) | 27 | ₹3,704 |
| August 2026 (Chennai joins) | 35 | ₹2,857 |

Per-rig cost drops as network grows; total pool stays at ₹1L. Strong incentive for partners to recruit.

**Prize distribution per class (₹50K each):**
- 1st: ₹30,000
- 2nd: ₹12,500
- 3rd: ₹7,500

**Local prize:** ₹500/rig per centre, paid to their centre's top qualifier.

## Eligibility & exclusivity
Entry into the league is controlled by [[thambu]]. Requirements:
- Hardware: triple monitor + 12 Newton metre load cell base minimum
- Software: [[uday]]'s VMS software
- Vision alignment with IERL's sim-to-reality concept

Not all centres supplied by [[thambu]] are IERL members — exclusivity is intentional for fair competition and uniform participant experience.

## Roles
- **[[thambu]]:** Gatekeeps entry, motorsport partnerships (ARKA, Buzzing Hornet, Coimbatore assets), training programs
- **[[uday]] / [[racing-point-esports]]:** VMS software, hot lap event technical execution and format
