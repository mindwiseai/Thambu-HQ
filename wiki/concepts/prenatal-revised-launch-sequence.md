# Prenatal Revised Launch Sequence (Scenario F)

A rejected launch scenario for [[prenatal-brand-name-the-edit|The Edit]]: launching with Her-only product at Day 1, deferring the Him formulation.

## Why it was rejected

Launching Her-only would directly contradict the [[prenatal-brand-manifesto-couples-equality|couples-equality manifesto]] — the brand's core position that TTC is a team job. Delaying Him by 6 months undercuts the founding commitment to reproductive partnership.

## What was chosen instead

Scenario D: couples bundle (Him + Her) at Day 1. See [[prenatal-launch-roadmap]] for the adopted architecture.
