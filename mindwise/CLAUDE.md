## Hard rules — DO NOT VIOLATE

1. **NO marketing claim without CTD backing.** Every clinical claim traces to a `../wiki/sources/` page → CTD Module 2. FSSAI liability is real.
2. **CDRI-08 ≠ generic Bacopa.** Generic is 20–30% bacosides; CDRI-08 is 55%+. Always distinguish.
3. **Voice/tone:** confident, educational, performance-driven, human. NEVER scary, medicinal, or prescription-like.
4. **Sorted Agency comms:** numbered feedback format (see `./Meeting Notes/message-to-binita-packaging-feedback.md` for canonical example).
5. **No single-bottle MRP discounting.** Bundle discounts only. Discounting destroys premium positioning.
