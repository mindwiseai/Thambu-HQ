# IERL Promotion Ladder & Fair Competition

How [[ierl-indian-esports-racing-league|IERL]] keeps the Amateur class competitive and stops one fast driver dominating forever. Decided 2026-06-14 ([[2026-06-14-ierl-july-launch]]).

## The two classes

- **Amateur** — Mazda MX-5 ND, ABS + traction control allowed. Default class, open to all.
- **Pro** — Ferrari 488 GT3, traction control disabled. Earned (or chosen).

## Promotion ladder

1. **Default Amateur** — every new driver starts here. No barrier.
2. **Voluntary Pro** — any driver may choose to race Pro anytime.
3. **Win Amateur network-wide** → automatic Pro promotion next month.
4. **Top 3 in Amateur twice** → mandatory Pro promotion (protects the Amateur field).
5. **Pro is permanent** — no downgrade, no sandbagging back to Amateur for easier prizes.

## Three layers of fair competition

1. **Promotion system** — dominant amateurs graduate within 1–2 months; the Amateur leaderboard stays winnable for newcomers.
2. **Local centre champion** — every centre crowns (and pays) its own fastest driver each month regardless of national rank. A customer who'll never be #1 nationally can still be champion of their city — a concrete reason to return. See [[ierl-direct-settlement]] for how the ₹500 × rigs local prize is paid.
3. **Track rotation from Season 2** — a [[mugello|Mugello]] specialist won't necessarily dominate the next track; rotation levels the field across the season.

**Framing:** the driver who keeps winning is a marketing asset, not a problem — "can you beat the record?" drives repeat visits.
