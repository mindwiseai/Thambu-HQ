# Curriculum — Track Day Programmes

**Separate from the Academy career path.** Chennai-specific, aimed at HNI supercar owners preparing for real circuit days at MMRT / Buddh / Coimbatore. Sessions are **90 minutes** (longer than Academy hours — these clients want depth and value per visit). 90%+ margin. See [[racesims-master-rate-card]].

> Positioning: not "learn to race." It's *"don't embarrass yourself — or bin your Lamborghini — on your first track day."* These owners currently have nowhere safe to prepare.

---

## Track Day Prep · 4 × 90 min · ₹25,000

For the first-time track-day participant. Get them safe, confident, and reference-equipped before they ever leave the pit lane in their own car.

### S1 — Why Sim Prep + MMRT Cold
- Why two hours in the sim saves the first (expensive, scary) session on track.
- **MMRT layout, corner by corner** — replicated in sim. Learn the circuit before you arrive.

### S2 — Braking & Reference Points
- **Threshold braking** in a high-power car; how much later a real supercar's brakes let you go (and the trap that sets).
- Fixed **braking markers** for every MMRT corner.

### S3 — Racing Line & Managing Power
- The **racing line** at MMRT; managing a powerful RWD car's exit without lighting up the rears.
- Smoothness as the fast (and safe) choice.

### S4 — Recovery, Safety & Full Lap
- **Spin recovery** (both feet in, where to look), flag rules, track etiquette, when to back off.
- Full-lap simulation tying it together.

**Outcome:** confident, reference-equipped, safety-aware — ready for a real track day.

---

## Advanced Performance · 6 × 90 min · ₹50,000

For the repeat track-day driver who wants to actually get *faster*, data-led.

### S1 — Reading Your Own Data
- Telemetry literacy: speed, brake, throttle traces — find your own time loss.

### S2 — Qualifying Pace vs Race Pace
- The one-lap maximum vs the sustainable, repeatable pace.

### S3 — Advanced Braking
- **Trail braking** in the real-car context; brake-release shape for rotation.

### S4 — Tyre Management & Consistency
- Keeping a real car's tyres alive over a 20-minute session; consistency as the real skill.

### S5 — Sector Optimisation at MMRT
- Data-led, sector by sector: where the tenths actually are.

### S6 — Mock Track-Day Session + Debrief
- A simulated session under realistic conditions; full telemetry debrief and a personal improvement plan.

**Outcome:** measurably faster, with a data-backed plan to keep improving on real track days.
