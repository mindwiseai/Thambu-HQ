# The Motorsport Academy

The structured training programme inside [[racesims-chennai-flagship|RaceSims Chennai]]. Three tiers — entry, advanced, and a real-car pathway. **The Academy is the *programme*, not the *place*** — the centre door says *RaceSims · Chennai*, the Academy lives inside it. See [[racesims-naming-architecture]]. Full pricing at [[racesims-master-rate-card]].

> *"India's motorsport academy. Built for racers who want a real seat at the end."*

---

## The three tiers

### Tier 1 — Initiation · ₹8,000

- **4 coached sessions · 1 hr each**
- For: anyone who wants to learn properly — first step on [[racesims-the-ladder|The Ladder]]
- Curriculum: sim fundamentals, racing line, braking zones, car control, timed assessment in session 4
- Outcome: **Initiation Licence** (lifetime) — unlocks Championship eligibility and the Advanced tier
- Completing Initiation is **Step 1 of The Ladder** — mandatory before competing

### Tier 2 — Advanced · ₹20,000 (Circuit) · ₹25,000 (Rally)

- **Circuit: 8 coached sessions · 1 hr each**
- **Rally: 10 coached sessions · 1 hr each**
- For: enthusiasts who want to race seriously and challenge for the Championship
- Circuit curriculum: race craft, overtaking, defensive driving, tyre management, advanced lines — on Le Mans Ultimate + iRacing
- Rally curriculum: pace notes, gravel/tarmac transitions, stage craft — on AC Rally + Richard Burns Rally · ARKA-endorsed syllabus
- Outcome: championship-ready driver · qualified to target Quarterly Shootouts and the Annual Grand Final
- Coaches review telemetry after every session

### Tier 3 — Sim + Reality · ₹1,75,000 (Rally) · ₹2,25,000 (Circuit)

The complete pathway — sim mastery leading to a real car on a real stage or circuit.

| | Rally | Circuit |
|--|-------|---------|
| **Sim prep** | 4 coached sessions (stage-specific technique) | 4 coached sessions (circuit-specific technique) |
| **Real car day** | ARKA Rally Drive — full stage in an ARKA-affiliated rally car | Buzzing Hornet — full race car experience at a real circuit |
| **Price** | **₹1,75,000** | **₹2,25,000** |
| **Our cost** | ₹1,00,000 (43% GM) | ₹1,25,000 (44% GM) |

Sold individually and as corporate/gifted experiences. The Annual Rally Champion and Annual Circuit Champion also **earn** their respective Real Drive through [[racesims-the-ladder|The Ladder]] — it is won, not purchased.

## What's NOT in the Academy

Two premium programmes exist but sit **outside** the Academy tier structure:

**Track Day Programmes** *(Chennai-specific · HNI supercar owners)*
For Lamborghini, Ferrari, Porsche, AMG owners preparing for MMRT / Buddh / Coimbatore track days. Not a motorsport career path — a performance prep service.
- Track Day Prep: 4 × 90 min · ₹25,000
- Advanced Performance: 6 × 90 min · ₹50,000

**Tuner Lab** *(corporate cohorts)*
Data-led vehicle dynamics + setup coaching for corporate teams. Not a racing progression — a team-building and engineering education product.
- 8 sessions × 3 hrs · ₹28,000/person · minimum 4 per cohort

Both are sold separately and are not part of the Ladder pathway.

## The customer journey

```
Walk in → Open Session (₹750/₹1,250)
         ↓  [staff upsell on 3rd visit]
Tier 1 → Initiation ₹8,000
         ↓  [earn licence, enter Championship]
Tier 2 → Advanced ₹20K/₹25K
         ↓  [win championship rounds]
         ↓  [Ladder: win Annual Grand Final]
Tier 3 → Sim + Reality ₹1.75L/₹2.25L  ← or earned as prize
         ↓
    Real motorsport seat
```

Most customers stop at Open Sessions. Some progress to Initiation. Fewer reach Advanced. The Sim + Reality tier is for the serious few — and one per year earns it for free.
