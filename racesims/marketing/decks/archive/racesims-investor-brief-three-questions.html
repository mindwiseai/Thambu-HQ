QUESTION ONE

What is RaceSims?

RaceSims is India's gateway to motorsport. Whether you want to try racing once or pursue it as a career — this is where you start.

India has thousands of motorsport aspirants — but unlike most other sports, the entry barrier into motorsport is excruciatingly high. Kart fees, equipment costs, training infrastructure, time: all gate the path to a real cockpit. Everyone aspires; few get the chance to try. RaceSims removes that gate. We make racing accessible for the price of a movie ticket, and we give those serious about it a structured ladder all the way to the grid.

Built across four lines of business:

01
RaceSims Experience Centres
Premium sim racing venues where anyone in India can come experience motorsport. Walk-in sessions · monthly memberships · corporate team events · private parties · café-lounge. Chennai flagship opens 2026, with a 43-centre national rollout over five years.
02
RaceSims Hardware Distribution
Official distributor and service partner in India for world-leading premium sim racing hardware brands. Bringing world-class racing equipment to Indian consumers, businesses, and our own centres — with full after-sales service.
03
RaceSims Motorsport Academy
Where Indians learn to race. Beginner courses for first-timers · progressive curriculum (Initiation → Race Academy → Rally School → Pro Track) for those serious about racing · Track Day Programmes for supercar owners preparing for real circuits · Real Driving Experiences combining simulator training with real-car days at the track · 1-on-1 coaching with race engineers · professional training for motorsport teams.
04
RaceSims Championships
India's first national-level sim racing leagues — both circuit and rally. The RaceSims Racing Championship and RaceSims Rally Championship launch 2027 · broadcast on digital platforms · sponsored by major brands · with structured seasons and prize structures. Top finishers earn opportunities to race at real motorsport events through our exclusive partnerships.

Founded by Thambu Samy, a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports — India's most-decorated motorsport team with 130+ national championship titles since 1987.
