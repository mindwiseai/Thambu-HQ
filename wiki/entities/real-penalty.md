# Real Penalty

A penalty / track-limits enforcement tool for [[assetto-corsa|Assetto Corsa]], used in online leagues. Enforces **strict track limits and automatic penalties** server-side (cut-track, invalid laps, etc.). Adopted by [[ierl-indian-esports-racing-league|IERL]] for Season 1.

## Why IERL uses it

- Vanilla Assetto Corsa track-limit enforcement is inconsistent. Real Penalty makes a cut lap automatically invalid — **no disputes**.
- Combined with **identical centre hardware** and the fact that all rigs are controlled by the centre (no home setups, no modified files), it creates a **controlled environment with zero scope for cheating**.
- This is the explicit reason IERL runs **in-centre, never from customers' homes**.
