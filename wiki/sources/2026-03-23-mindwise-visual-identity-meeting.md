## Summary

Sorted Agency presented **three visual identity routes** for Mindwise, each with 2–3 expression options, for a total of **4 logo concepts** lock-in. The session explored how to move from a **clinical/pharmaceutical visual language** to a **performance-oriented, modern identity** that reflects the brand's positioning as a high-performer's daily advantage, not a corrective supplement.

All four concepts tested well with internal stakeholders. Route 3 ("Architecture of Intelligence") emerged as the strongest, with neon-green + black color treatment preferred for maximum shelf/social impact.

---

## Key Facts

### Brand positioning recap
- **Core promise:** "Your Unfair Advantage" — only government-backed daily brain supplement for sharper mind
- **How it works:** Strengthens neurons, builds neural links → brain performs at full potential
- **Key shift:** Technical/clinical promise → **promise of leverage** (daily advantage, not a treatment for decline)
- **Target:** High performers, growth mindset (WHOOP/Nike/Coded adjacency, not pharma)

### Visual identity mandate
The logo **must reflect the shift from clinical to performance:**
- ❌ Move away: generic, archaic, forgettable, mundane, dull, templatized
- ✅ Move toward: assertive, optimization-oriented (not harmony), pioneering, iconic, active body language

**Brand body-language scale:**
- Quiet Confidence → **Assertive**
- Credibility implied → **Credibility upfront**
- Harmonious → **Optimization** (upgrade that pushes further)
- Pioneering focus on innovation → **Rooted in heritage** (innovation + purity)
- Intimate/relatable → **Intimate** (kept)
- Energized calm → **Active intensity** (contrast, movement, momentum)

---

## Three Visual Routes (4 options)

### Route 1A: "The Peak Mind" — Expansion variant
**Philosophy:** For people chasing mastery and flow; mountain peak/pyramid metaphor for elevation and ascent.

**Design:**
- Typography: structured, powerful, confident foundation
- Form expands **outward on both sides** → suggests elevation, progress, achievement through expansion
- Mixed case: uppercase for structure + lowercase for fluidity + 3D appeal
- Metaphor: mountain peak → ambition → pursuit of peak cognitive performance

**Insignia:** M + W combined with separate treatment (not overlapped)

**Color:** Neon green + black (strong preference noted — "instant pop," "magnetic pull," reminiscent of Gatorade for the mind)

**Reaction:**
- "Goes fluently together"
- "Goal-driven, aspirational, go-getter spirit"
- "Takes liberty to misbehave, takes space in world of well-behaved pharma brands"
- **Internal assessment:** Bold, but "intimidating" to some (Tanya: one A is too bold but 1B calms that)

---

### Route 1B: "The Stoic Mind" — Restraint variant
**Philosophy:** Real performance doesn't flash; it reveals itself through restraint. Clarity, control, composure come from consistency, not hype.

**Design:**
- Typography: clean, grounded, minimal — allows weight and structure to carry identity
- **No expansion effect** vs 1A
- Still bold, confident, "can't take this brand for granted"
- Personality: calm, controlled, quietly powerful

**Insignia:** M + W **joined together** as overlapping zigzag pattern (vs 1A separate M/W)
- Represents cohesive thinking that peak-performing brain is capable of

**Color:** Same neon green + black as 1A (but colors differentiated subtly across expressions)

**Reaction:**
- "Standing tall and definitely yeah"
- "Flows fluently together"
- "Quietly standing out" (vs boldly standing out)
- **Internal assessment:** Less intimidating than 1A; still powerful

---

### Route 2: "The Neural Network"
**Philosophy:** Inspired by brain scans — billions of neurons communicating in milliseconds; every thought/memory/decision is connections firing in synchrony.

**Design:**
- Typography: **lowercase**, tightly interconnected, **overlapping** (like neurons forming pathways)
- Letters "lean into" each other → visual representation of constant communication
- Spark metaphor: dots on 'i' represent neural sparks firing
- Effect: letters feel "alive" with neural activity, not static

**Insignia:** M transformed with break/spark + exclamation mark-like ending
- "Spark that gets it all going"
- Works as both spark AND bold marker for sharpness

**Color:** Option shown in darker green (vs neon)

**Reaction:**
- Strongest emotional consensus issue: **concern about legibility/comprehension in-store**
  - Transcript: "I don't know how well it's going to be perceived in terms of the idea that we've gone behind to put this logo together... people picking up a bottle in a store are going to have when they see it."
  - Counterpoint: interactive website + animation will bring concept to life; static mockups can't convey interactivity
- "Intuitive to how brain functions"
- Challenge flagged: **communicating the concept without website context** (retail/pharma environment)

---

### Route 3: "The Architecture of Intelligence" ⭐ Strongest
**Philosophy:** The brain is the foundation of everything — ambitions, ideas, progress. Without a healthy brain, none of it stands. Frame brain as **architectural structure** (strong, foundational, essential).

**Design:**
- **Insignia:** M **transforms into brain silhouette** (not a literal drawing; geometric, modern interpretation)
  - Hints at letter M while resembling protective roof-like form
  - Reveals brain silhouette → layered metaphor
  - Works as standalone icon
- Typography: **Helvetica Neue** (clarity + timelessness)
  - "Mind" in **bold** (strength)
  - "wise" in **lighter weight** (fluidity, wisdom)
  - Creates visual balance: strength + subtlety
- "Mind-wise" compound word (vs split) makes it more legible, easier to read as single entity

**Insignia philosophy:** Protects and gets best out of your mind

**Color:** Neon green + black (requested as option)

**Reaction:** 
- 🌟 **Universal strong preference** — "all of us universally had this was the third one"
- "It feels smart"
- "More intrigued" vs intimidated
- "Ingenious" how geometric M transforms to brain shape without cliché
- Legible, instantly legible
- "Has a punch but also sense of maturity"
- **Internal consensus:** This is it. Others help you understand the journey; this is the obvious answer.

---

## Decisions Made

1. **Direction lock:** Route 3 ("Architecture of Intelligence") selected for next phase
2. **Color:** Neon green + black confirmed as primary (all other combinations to be prepared as options but neon is the lock)
3. **Insignia:** M-to-brain transformation (Route 3)
4. **Typography:** Helvetica Neue, Mind (bold) + wise (light)

## Design Process Notes

**How 7 routes were filtered to 4:**
- Initial 7 routes created by Sorted team
- Filtered via external feedback loop: non-team designers asked to rate each route with single feeling-word (not good/bad), without explanation
- Rating system applied → removed low-signal options
- 4 winners presented (in no particular order per team, though 3 emerged strongest)

**Legibility strategy:**
- "mind-wise" (compound) reads better than "mind wise" (split)
- Bold-light weight treatment helps scanability
- Geometric brain > literal brain drawing (avoids cliché in category)
