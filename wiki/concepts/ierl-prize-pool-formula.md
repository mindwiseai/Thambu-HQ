## Formula
**Pool ÷ total pods = per-pod fee.** The pool is set per round; per-pod cost drops as the network grows, so partners benefit when more centres join. Fees are rounded to clean numbers; any overage is retained by the last centre in the settlement chain.

Default target pool is ₹1,00,000. **July 2026 launches at a ₹50,000 pool** (soft launch).
