## Overview
Buzzing Hornet Motorsports is a motorsport company in Chennai that [[thambu]] works with. They operate a Volkswagen Polo Cup car and offer real track experiences.

**Location:** Chennai
**Car:** Volkswagen Polo Cup

## IERL role
Buzzing Hornet Motorsports is one of the real-world assets that sits on top of the [[ierl]] sim-to-reality vision. IERL drivers can earn track experiences on the VW Polo Cup car at Chennai.

This is the Chennai-based real-car touchpoint for the program (complementing [[arka-motorsports]]'s Coimbatore assets).
