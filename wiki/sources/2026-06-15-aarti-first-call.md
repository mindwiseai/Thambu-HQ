# Aarti First Call — Sorted Agency Discovery

Foundational discovery call between [[thambu]] and [[aarti-samant]] ([[sorted-agency]]). First strategic consultation on Mindwise brand positioning and communication.

## Summary

65-minute consultation where [[thambu]] explains the complete [[cdri-08]] story to [[aarti-samant]]. Aarti delivers blunt feedback: the brand looks too clinical/medical. She recommends brand communication strategy before execution, emphasizing WHY/WHAT/HOW framework and shifting from "clinical" to "human" personality (Whoop/SuperYou positioning, not pharma).

## Key Facts

- **Product:** [[cdri-08]] Brahmi extract, CDRI-patented process, 55% bacosides
- **Clinical backing:** 10-15 years research, multiple trials
- **Global brands:** Memory Plus (India 1990s, Vishwanathan ambassador) → [[keenmind]] (Australia) → Memo Plus Gold (Philippines)
- **Adam Ritson validation:** Australian brain injury case → government-funded neuroimaging studies
- **Contract status:** SFI distributor agreement expired ~6-12 months prior; India market now free
- **Current state:** mindwise-cdri.com, @mindwise.cdri Instagram, minimal traction, clinical positioning

## Decisions Made

1. **Engage [[sorted-agency]] for brand communication strategy** (3-part: workshop, content, design)
2. **Remove CDRI from brand name** — CDRI moves to HOW (credibility), not WHO (identity)
3. **Delete current Instagram content** — restart with fresh strategy
4. **Logo refresh required** — too clinical
5. **[[thambu]] as founder/creator** — use authentic dyslexic→confident story
6. **Target #studygram influencers** (20k-50k, exam students, morning routine format)

## Strategic Issues Identified

### The Problem
- Current Instagram feels clinical, not human — trust critical for brain health
- 80% of content job is the hook (first 3 seconds); current posts lose attention
- Brand personality missing; looks like medical flyer, not creator/lifestyle brand
- Comparison: Whoop, Well-Being Nutrition succeed via personality, not clinical positioning

### The Positioning Gap
- **Enemy brand:** NeuroGum (quick 4-hour energy fix, caffeine+L-theanine)
- **True positioning:** Long-term cognitive building (vs. shortcuts like Ozempic, Temple brain device)
- **Needed essence:** Safe, trusted, clever (Indian Ayurveda + modern science)
- **[[thambu]]'s narrative:** Dyslexic → confident high performer (powerful, authentic)

## Action Items

**From Sorted Agency (3-part engagement):**
1. Brand Communication Workshop — WHY/WHAT/HOW, enemy, manifesto, tagline, personality
2. Content Strategy — influencer, Instagram organic, paid ads frameworks
3. Design/Identity System — logo, color palette, social media consistency

**From Mindwise:**
- Answer 3 fundamental questions: Why does Mindwise exist? What is it? How do you deliver?

- Decide execution model: internal creative + agency, or full delegation
- Ongoing: consult Sorted every ~2 months post-strategy
