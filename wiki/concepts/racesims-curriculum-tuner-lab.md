# Curriculum — Tuner Lab

**Separate from the Academy career path. Corporate / engineering programme.**
**8 sessions × 3 hrs · ₹28,000/person · minimum 4 per cohort**

Vehicle dynamics and car setup taught as both real engineering and a leadership/teamwork metaphor: *gather data → form a hypothesis → make one change → test → iterate.* The same loop that wins races runs a good team. Appeals to corporate buyers who want team-building *with substance*, and to engineering-minded enthusiasts.

> The hook: every other corporate offsite is paintball or an escape room. This one teaches your team how engineers actually solve problems — and ends in a race.

---

## S1 — Vehicle Dynamics 101
What makes a car fast: the **contact patch**, the friction circle, and **weight transfer** under braking, cornering, and acceleration. The mental model everything else hangs on.

## S2 — The Tyre
The only thing touching the road. Grip vs **slip angle**, operating temperature, and how **pressure** changes the contact patch. Why the tyre is the centre of every setup decision.

## S3 — Springs, Dampers & Ride
Mechanical grip and body control. Springs hold the car up; dampers control *how fast* weight transfers. Soft vs stiff, and what each does to feel and grip.

## S4 — Anti-Roll Bars & Balance
The primary balance tool: tuning **understeer vs oversteer** front-to-rear with ARBs. Drivers feel the same car turned from a pushy understeerer into a pointy oversteerer.

## S5 — Aero & Gearing
Downforce vs drag — the trade you make for your circuit. Gear ratios matched to the track's longest straight and slowest corner.

## S6 — Alignment
**Camber, toe, caster** — what each does to grip, tyre wear, and stability. The fine-tuning layer.

## S7 — Data-Driven Setup
Reading telemetry to *diagnose* a problem (is that understeer mechanical or driver-induced?). The diagnostic loop: **hypothesis → one change → test → compare**. Never change two things at once.

## S8 — Team Setup Challenge
Teams are given a poorly-set-up car and a clock. Using everything learned, they diagnose, tune, and hand it to a driver for a race-off against the other teams. Full debrief framed as the leadership metaphor: data over opinion, disciplined iteration, clear roles, calm under time pressure.

**Outcome:** a team that has visibly solved a hard technical problem together — and the winning car to prove it.
