Part one · The opportunity

What is RaceSims?

Indian motorsport is in a moment most of us have waited a long time for. F1 viewership in India crossed 50 million in the last season, growing 25%+ year on year. The INRC delivers season after season of close racing. Teams like ARKA Motorsports have spent four decades building championship pedigree that quietly rivals anything in Asia. The audience is there. The talent is there.

What's been missing is two things at once.

For the millions of people who'd love to try motorsport — to feel the wheel, the brakes, a hot lap — there's been no easy way in. Padel courts, cricket nets, climbing gyms, tennis clubs have made trying a sport feel casual and affordable. Motorsport hasn't had that. The cost of entry has kept it out of reach.

And for those who want to take it further — to develop the craft and one day race for real — the ladder up has been narrow, expensive, and gated for too long.

RaceSims is built for both. A place where anyone can try motorsport for the price of a movie ticket, and where those serious about it can find a real path to the racing grid.

We are India's integrated motorsport development platform — operating premium centres, distributing the best sim racing hardware, hosting national championships, and through partnerships with ARKA and Polo Cup, putting top graduates into real-world seats. We're not replacing the motorsport ecosystem. We're adding a new on-ramp to it — one wide enough for casual curiosity, steep enough for serious ambition.

Three things make this possible now

01 · The equipment
Direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, and load-cell pedals — the same technology behind F1 driver simulators — has finally become affordable enough to deploy commercially in India. A full premium rig that cost ₹25 lakh five years ago is now in the ₹6-10 lakh range. The technology has caught up to the ambition.
02 · The pedigree
As a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports (130+ national championship titles since 1987), every curriculum, every coaching session, every centre standard is shaped by people who have stood on real race grids. RaceSims is sim racing built by motorsport people. Top Motorsport Academy graduates earn real-world seat opportunities through our partnerships with ARKA and the Polo Cup ecosystem.
03 · The integration
RaceSims is one of two distributors in India for both Conspit and VNM — the world's leading premium sim racing brands. The distribution rights give us favourable hardware economics on every centre we build. But the deeper edge isn't the kit — it's that we operate the full development stack under one brand: centres + hardware + championship + curriculum + sim-to-real pathway. Everyone else in this market plays one part of that. We play all of them.

RaceSims is three businesses under one brand. We operate premium sim racing centres — the flagship opens in Chennai in 2026, with a 43-centre national rollout mapped over five years. We distribute Conspit and VNM hardware nationally. And we organise the National Sim Racing Championship — launching in 2027, with broadcast, sponsors, and a real seat at the end for the season's best.

Inside every centre, Open Sessions (drop-in, ₹500-1,000/hour, walk-in welcome) sit alongside The Motorsport Academy — the structured curriculum from Initiation through Race Academy, Rally School, and Pro Track for those who want a serious path. The Ladder takes top Academy graduates the rest of the way — through ARKA Rally Drive and Polo Cup Follow Car — to real-world racing seats.

Part two · Where in three years

The market and the position we're building.

There's a particular kind of dream that nearly every Indian teenager has held at some point — racing driver. India has 425 million gamers, with 10 million+ playing racing games regularly — F1 Mobile, Asphalt, Real Racing, the F1 23 and Assetto Corsa community. Drive to Survive sits among Netflix India's most-watched non-fiction series. The dream is widespread. The audience is real.

What's been missing is an affordable way in. By May 2029, RaceSims will be two things, equally important — and each one is its own market.

Pillar one

The most accessible motorsport experience in India.

The premise is simple. Anyone curious about motorsport — a teenager, a corporate team, a couple, a serious gamer who has never sat in a real cockpit — should be able to try it for the price of a movie ticket.

What that means in practice
  • Open Sessions at ₹500-1,000/hour · walk-in welcome · no commitment
  • Memberships at ₹5,000-9,000/month for regulars (Pass 8 and Pass 16)
  • Group, corporate, and private hire from ₹15,000-60,000 per booking — experiential events with real production value
  • World-class equipment — direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, load-cell pedals · the same hardware F1 driver-development simulators use, now in Indian centres
  • Café-lounge experience · premium service, branded environment, suitable for a date as much as a corporate off-site
Why it works as a category

Padel, cricket nets, climbing gyms, tennis clubs have made trying their sports casual and affordable in India. Motorsport has remained out of reach — cost-gated, family-gated, equipment-gated. A premium consumer sim rig still costs ₹6-10 lakh to own. RaceSims spreads that cost across thousands of customers per centre per year.

For ₹500 per hour, the consumer gets ₹40 lakh worth of equipment, coaching available on request, and a real motorsport context. Nowhere else in India can deliver that.

Market size

The Indian premium experiential market — boutique fitness, premium gaming, immersive entertainment — is growing 20-30% a year. Smaaash, Bowl-O-Mania, Padel HQ have raised significant capital in adjacent categories. Motorsport sits beside all of them with a deeper cultural pull (F1, MotoGP, INRC, Drive to Survive) and no equivalent operator at scale today.

Revenue contribution

By Year 5, the Drive pillar — Open Sessions, memberships, group bookings, café, merch — contributes ~78% of revenue at a mature centre (₹1.97 cr of ₹2.52 cr). This is the volume engine. Across 43 centres at saturation: a network throughput of 100,000+ unique customers per year.

Pillar two

The platform where Indian motorsport development starts.

The second pillar is what makes RaceSims different from every other premium experience operator. For those serious about motorsport, the structured pathway is here. Not as an upsell — as the centre's whole reason to exist.

The Motorsport Academy · curriculum-led, instructor-led
  • Initiation · 4 sessions · ₹8,000 — first-timers learning racing fundamentals properly
  • Race Academy · 8 sessions · ₹14,000 — racecraft, overtaking, racing line, telemetry analysis
  • Rally School · 10 sessions · ₹16,000 — rally fundamentals, pace notes, surface management
  • Pro Track · bespoke · ongoing — tailored programmes for drivers preparing for real racing
  • 1-on-1 coaching with ARKA-experienced instructors · ₹2,500-3,500/hour
Real driving experiences · the bridge from sim to real

Through partnerships with ARKA Motorsports (₹1.75 lakh per package) and Buzzing Hornet (₹2.25 lakh per package), customers move from a sim morning at our centre to a real-car afternoon at the track. This product exists only through RaceSims — no other Indian operator has these supplier relationships.

Pro Driver Training · B2B for real motorsport teams

Sim sessions and telemetry coaching for active motorsport teams — pre-event preparation, simulator-based testing, race-weekend support. The same race-engineering toolkit ARKA uses on its real cars, delivered to other teams. Per-event or retainer pricing.

The RaceSims Championship · launching 2027

Broadcast, sponsor packages, documented prize structure — including real-world race seats at the end. By Y3, the championship runs across 17 centres with regional qualifiers and a national final.

The Ladder · the integrated sim-to-real pathway

Initiation → Race Academy → RaceSims Championship → Real Driving Experience → ARKA Rally Drive or Polo Cup Follow Car. Each tier earns the next. Specific drivers — not hypothetical, trackable — will graduate through this pathway. By Year 3, the first Academy graduate is on the grid of a real Indian motorsport round.

Why it's defensible

The pedigree moat is real and unique. RaceSims is founded by a working race engineer at ARKA — India's most-decorated motorsport team with 130+ national championship titles since 1987. Every curriculum, coaching session, and centre standard is shaped by people who have stood on real race grids. Top Motorsport Academy graduates earn real-world seat opportunities through partnerships with ARKA and the Polo Cup ecosystem. No other Indian brand can offer this. No other Indian brand even has the option.

Revenue contribution

By Y5, the Train and Compete pillars contribute ~22% of centre revenue (₹55 lakh of ₹2.52 cr) but deliver greater than 50% of the brand value, defensibility, and sponsorship attractiveness. This is the depth engine — sticky customers, premium pricing, real outcomes, and the only stream that compounds across the network (championship hosting, leaderboards, broadcast).
