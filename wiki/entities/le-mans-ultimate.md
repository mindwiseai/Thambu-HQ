# Le Mans Ultimate (LMU)

Endurance racing simulator. The **planned migration target** for [[ierl-indian-esports-racing-league|IERL]] once the league's software is ready — IERL starts on [[assetto-corsa|Assetto Corsa]] and moves to LMU later.

## Status: future, not July

- **July launch is on [[assetto-corsa|Assetto Corsa]]**, not LMU. LMU 24/7 hosting (RaceControl.gg, per-minute billing ≈ ₹19,500/month) was unviable for an always-on leaderboard; AC self-hosts cheaply.
- Also aligns with the LOCKED [[racesims-championship-architecture]], which names LMU + iRacing for the in-centre Chennai championship — the platform mismatch flagged in [[2026-06-14-ierl-july-launch]] resolves as IERL eventually converging on LMU.

## Migration implication

LMU ties identity more tightly to the online account, so per-customer name-swapping on a shared rig is harder than in AC. The [[ierl-driver-identification|kiosk's name-injection bridge]] therefore matters **more** after migration — the move becomes mostly a change of *where* the driver name is written.
