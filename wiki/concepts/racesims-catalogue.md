# RaceSims Catalogue 2026

The complete RaceSims product line organised the way a race engineer thinks about a build: **controls first** (where the driver actually interacts with the machine), **cockpit second** (the seat and frame that absorbs the inputs), **display and compute third** (what the driver sees and the brain behind it), **audio + haptics fourth** (everything that makes the cabin feel real), **accessories last**, and **preconfigured bundles** at the end for customers who'd rather not assemble decisions one by one.

Read this from top to bottom for the first time. Use the Table of Contents at the bottom to navigate later.

> **Pricing discipline:** every price in this document is **MRP in INR**, current as of May 2026 unless noted. Prices marked `[verify]` should be confirmed against the live pricelist or supplier quote at the time of customer quote — landed costs and supplier MRPs shift quarterly. See [[kit-pricing]] for the source spreadsheet reference.

## How RaceSims approaches a build

Most sim-racing retailers sell components. RaceSims sells *systems* — every kit is built around the principle that **the driver's connection to the car is in the controls and the seat, and everything else exists to support that connection.** Three things that show up in the catalogue because of it:

1. **The wheelbase is the spine of the build.** Pick this first; everything else slots in to match its capability.
2. **The pedals are where most lap time lives.** Cheap pedals undercut expensive wheelbases. We don't sell mismatched stacks.
3. **Audio + haptics is not an afterthought.** It's a separate tier with its own decisions, locked in [[racesims-audio-tiers]].

The race-engineer voice in this catalogue: every product entry answers "**why a race engineer specs this part**", not "what gaming influencers say is good." See [[race-engineer-positioning]] for the full positioning framework.

## Hard rules / catalogue maintenance

- Every price marked `[verify]` must be confirmed against the live pricelist or supplier quote before customer quote. **Never quote MRPs from this document directly without verification.**
- Brand names are non-negotiable: **RaceSims** (one word), **Conspit**, **VNM**, **Simagic**, **Sonos**, **Audeze Maxwell**, **Dayton BTS-1**.
- Per [[race-engineer-positioning]]: don't produce listing copy that could be written by a generic sim-racing reseller. Every product entry must answer "why a race engineer specs this."
- This catalogue lives in the wiki as the canonical reference. Customer-facing PDF / web versions are produced from this source — when this changes, downstream collateral changes too.
- [[customer-pipeline]] tracks who's been quoted at each tier — log every active conversation against a tier so we know what mix is actually selling.
