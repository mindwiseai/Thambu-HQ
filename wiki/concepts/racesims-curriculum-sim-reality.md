# Curriculum — Sim + Reality

**Tier 3 · 4 sim-prep sessions + 1 real-car day**
**Rally (ARKA) ₹1,75,000 · Circuit (Buzzing Hornet) ₹2,25,000**

The pinnacle of the Academy: translate hard-won sim skill into a real car, safely and effectively. Sold as a premium package and **earned** by the Annual Champion via [[racesims-the-ladder|The Ladder]] (won, not bought). The structure is identical across disciplines; only the car, venue, and partner change.

> The whole product rests on one truth RaceSims can prove: *every driver who does structured sim prep outperforms one who walks in cold.* This programme is that proof, sold.

---

## The four prep sessions (sim)

### Prep 1 — The Real Car
- What changes sim → real: **g-forces**, fear, fatigue, no reset button, the cost of a mistake. The mindset shift.
- The specific car: an ARKA-affiliated rally car / a Buzzing Hornet race car — specs, controls, quirks. Set expectations.

### Prep 2 — Learn the Venue Cold
- The actual stage / circuit (e.g. MMRT for circuit) replicated in sim.
- Drive it until it's memorised — every corner, marker, surface change. Walk in knowing the road.

### Prep 3 — Technique Transfer
- The techniques that matter most in *this* car on *this* venue. What translates 1:1, what feels different in reality.
- Reference points that hold up in the real world (braking markers you'll actually see).

### Prep 4 — Safety & The Plan
- Real-motorsport safety: HANS, harness, fire systems, marshals, flags, comms. Rally: co-driver protocol.
- The minute-by-minute plan for the real day. What "good" looks like; what to do if it goes wrong.

## The real-car day

```
MORNING                          AFTERNOON
─────────                        ─────────
Sim warm-up on the venue   →     Real car, real venue
Final brief with the team        ARKA team / Buzzing Hornet
Safety + scrutineering           Instructor alongside / lead car
                                 Full safety crew
                          →      Telemetry captured
                          →      Video + onboard
```

- **Rally:** a full stage in an ARKA-affiliated rally car, with the ARKA team and [[mr-leela-krishnan|Mr. Leelakrishnan]]'s crew.
- **Circuit:** real laps in a Buzzing Hornet race car at a circuit, with their team.
- **The debrief that sells the brand:** sim trace vs real trace, side by side. Proof the prep worked. The video becomes content (and, for the Champion, the [[racesims-the-ladder|Ladder]] hero piece).
