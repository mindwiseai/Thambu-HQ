1. Welcome & Why This Brief

Welcome to the RaceSims partnership. This document is your single source of truth for everything Instagram. It's intentionally opinionated — the goal is to give you enough context that you can ship great work without bouncing every decision back to us.

Read it once cover-to-cover before drafting your first post. After that, treat sections 4 (voice) and 5 (visual) as your daily checklist.

3. The Moat — Read This Twice

The Single Unfair Advantage

Thambu is the active race engineer for Arka Motorsports — India's most decorated motorsport team, with 130+ national championship titles since 1987. He works directly with N. Leelakrishnan, a 7-time National Rally Champion with 35+ years in Indian motorsports.

Arka is currently the factory rally team for Mahindra and runs Volkswagen Polo R2s on MRF tyres. Their drivers — Karna Kadur & Kumar Ramaswamy — just won both APRC Round 1 and INRC Round 1 at Chennai (April 2026).

This is the single thing competitors cannot copy. VRH cannot fake race-engineer credibility. They can't write about telemetry, setup engineering, or sim-to-real transfer authentically. We can.

Every Instagram post should carry this DNA. Not as a hashtag — as the perspective behind the content.

Test it: If a post could have been written by anyone with a Google search, rewrite it. If it requires a race-engineer's perspective to make, ship it.

4. Voice & Tone

Voice Pillars

PillarWhat It Means

Technical, but humanUse racing engineering language (FFB, brake bias, slip angle, telemetry) but always translate. We're educating, not gatekeeping.
Confident, not boastful"Built by a race engineer" — not "the best in India". Let the credibility speak for itself.
India-firstIndian buyers, Indian motorsport context, INR pricing, local logistics. Reference Indian rallies, Indian drivers, Indian championships.
Premium, never cheapPro-grade hardware. We don't compete on price. Tone matches that — no "limited time offer!!!" energy.

Two-Column Voice Check

Sounds Like Us ✓

  • "Why a race engineer chooses Conspit pedals — telemetry shows brake-pressure curves map almost 1:1 with real hydraulic pedals."
  • "Setup engineering for sim drivers: how camber and toe transfer between sim and real."
  • "Behind the scenes at Arka — how telemetry from a real rally car informs how we tune our rigs."
  • "Karna Kadur trains on a RaceSims rig between rallies. Here's why."

Doesn't Sound Like Us ✗

  • "BEST sim racing setup 🔥🔥🔥 DM us!!!"
  • "Top 5 sim racing tips you NEED to know" (generic listicle)
  • "Compare us to [competitor name]" (we never punch sideways)
  • "Cheapest sim rig in India" (we don't compete on price)
  • Heavy emoji walls. Two emojis max per caption.

Caption Structure

  • Hook (line 1): A specific, sharp claim — not a question. "Most sim drivers brake too late on entry." Not "Are you braking too late on entry?"
  • Body (2–4 lines): The technical insight. Telemetry, mechanics, race-engineer reasoning.
  • Tie-in (1 line): How RaceSims hardware enables this — without being a sales pitch.
  • CTA: Soft. "DM for a setup walkthrough." "Comment your home track and I'll send a setup tip." Avoid "BUY NOW".
  • Hashtags: 8–12 tags, motorsport-focused. See section 9 for the standard set.

Color usage rules

  • Red is the RaceSims accent. Use it sparingly — for CTAs, brand mark moments, key highlights.
  • Gold is for victory / championship moments only. Don't use it on regular product posts.
  • Backgrounds default to Race Black or Deep Navy. Avoid pure white backgrounds.
  • Never use teal/cyan — that's Mindwise (the founder's other brand). Keep them separate.

Typography

Photography

  • Real motorsport > stock images. Always.
  • Real rally cars (Arka VW Polos), real drivers (Karna, Leelakrishnan), real workshops, real sim rigs in real environments.
  • Heavy darks, high contrast. Strong gradients on lower thirds for text legibility.
  • Avoid shiny showroom shots, generic stock racing wheels, esports/gaming setups.

Logo Use

  • RaceSims wordmark on dark backgrounds: white version
  • RaceSims wordmark on light backgrounds: black version
  • When co-branding with Arka or partners, use the format: ARKA × RACESIMS × MRF with × separators
  • Logos always with breathing room (min 15px padding). Never crop, rotate, or recolor.

6. Content Categories & Cadence

We're aiming for 4–5 posts per week across feed + reels, plus daily stories. Mix categories — never two of the same type back-to-back.

2× per week
Race-Engineer Insights

Technical breakdowns. Telemetry, setup tips, sim-to-real transfer, hardware reasoning. The signature content. Reels preferred.

1× per week
Product Showcase

Hardware close-ups, build process, unboxings. Cinematic, dark, slow-motion. Always tie back to "why a race engineer chose this."

1× per week
Arka / Real Racing

Behind-the-scenes from rallies, workshop content, Karna Kadur training, Leelakrishnan moments. The credibility layer.

As needed
Race Day / Victory

Win posts, podium content, championship updates. Use the gold + dark navy template (see Section 8 — INRC R1 example).

1× per week
Customer / Community

Customer rigs in their setups, testimonials, race results from RaceSims customers. Social proof.

Daily
Stories

Behind-the-scenes, polls, quick takes, race weekend updates. Less polished, more frequent. Keep them on-brand though.

7. The Hard Rules

Always Do

  • Write "RaceSims" as one word. Always.
  • Reference the race-engineer/Arka credibility somewhere in every post — caption, image, or both.
  • Tag @arkamotorsport, @karnakadur, brand partners (@conspitofficial, @vnm.cockpits, @mozaracing) when relevant.
  • Use INR for all pricing.
  • Send drafts for review before posting (workflow in section 10).

Never Do

  • Mention or compare directly to Virtual Racing Hub or any competitor by name. Position on our strengths, never against theirs.
  • Use generic sim racing stock content. The whole point is that ours is different.
  • Promise driver/Arka quotes without our approval. We control how Karna and Leelakrishnan are quoted.
  • Post the Mindwise (cognitive supplement) brand colors or messaging. That's a separate company.
  • Use AI-generated logos. Always pull from the asset folder (section 11).
  • Run paid promotions, giveaways, or partnerships without sign-off.
