# Uday — Hyderabad Dealer

RaceSims **dealer in Hyderabad** (bought rigs from [[racesims-company|RaceSims]]) — **network partner, not competition.** His operating data is the closest thing to a real-world proof point for the [[racesims-chennai-premium-baseline|Chennai centre economics]].

## Operating facts (as of 2026-05-29, via [[thambu]])
- **8 rigs.**
- **Charges ₹800/hour.**
- **Peak revenue ~₹4.5L/month** — reached in his **6th month** of operation, **still climbing** when he paused.
- Implied occupancy at peak: ₹4.5L ÷ ₹800 ÷ (8×12×30) ≈ **~20%** at month 6.
- **Overhead ~₹2.5L/month:** rent ₹1.6L · electricity ₹45k · 2 staff (1/shift) ₹50k. Note: he does **not** book a rig-maintenance/replacement reserve — under-reserved, not truly leaner.
- **Shut his store** to develop the [[racesims-vms-build|Venue Management Software]] — which RaceSims (and the network) will use. Took the downtime and dev cost; Chennai gets the tool.

## Why he matters
- His ~20%-by-month-6 ramp is the **empirical anchor** for the [[racesims-chennai-premium-baseline]] forecast — it reality-checked the [[racesims-chennai-flagship]] v3.1 deck's 30-68% occupancy assumptions as optimistic.
- His 2-staff model proves an 8-rig centre runs lean — basis for Chennai's "open on 2 staff" recommendation.
- **Action for Thambu:** get Uday's full 6-month playbook (marketing, customer mix, peak hours, [[racesims-championship-architecture|Hot Lap]] impact). Single biggest free de-risker on the Chennai plan.
