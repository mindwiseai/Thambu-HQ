# Curriculum — Advanced Rally

**Tier 2 · 10 sessions × 1 hr · ₹25,000 · requires Initiation Licence**

Takes a licensed driver to competitive rally driver. Platforms: **Assetto Corsa Rally + Richard Burns Rally**. **ARKA-endorsed syllabus** — [[mr-leela-krishnan|Mr. Leelakrishnan]]'s 7× national-champion technique informs the stage craft. Runs the [[racesims-academy-curriculum|B·D·A·D model]].

> Two extra sessions vs Circuit because rally adds two whole skill domains the circuit never touches: **the loose surface** and **the pace-note system**.

---

## S1 — Rally Fundamentals & The Loose Surface

- **Brief:** Grip on gravel/loose vs tarmac — the car moves around under you; **slip angle** is your friend. Commitment and looking far ahead.
- **Drill:** Gravel handling; feel the slide and catch it.

## S2 — Weight Transfer & The Scandinavian Flick

- **Brief:** The **Scandinavian flick** — a deliberate pendulum weight transfer to rotate the car *before* a corner, using trailing-throttle oversteer.
- **Drill:** Flick into a series of corners.

## S3 — Left-Foot Braking

- **Brief:** **Left-foot braking** — adjust the car's attitude mid-corner without lifting the throttle, keeping the turbo spooled and balancing the car.
- **Drill:** LFB through linked corners.

## S4 — The Pace Note System I

- **Brief:** **Pace notes** — the numbering system (1 = tightest → 6 = flat-out), distances ("100"), links ("into"), and modifiers (long, tightens, opens, *don't cut*, caution, crest, jump).
- **Drill:** Drive a known stage calling your own notes aloud.

## S5 — The Pace Note System II + Co-Driver

- **Brief:** The *rhythm* of notes — reading ahead, the timing of the call, and the trust between driver and co-driver.
- **Drill:** Blind stage driven entirely on a co-driver's calls — the real rally experience.

## S6 — Surface & Condition Reading

- **Brief:** Swept gravel (grippy line vs marbles off-line), ruts, mud, tarmac transitions, rain and night running.
- **Drill:** Mixed-surface stage.

## S7 — Jumps, Crests & Hazards

- **Brief:** Car control over **jumps** (no braking airborne; set attitude before take-off), blind **crests**, and *don't-cut* hazards (rocks, ditches).
- **Drill:** Stage with jumps and blind crests.

## S8 — Stage Craft & Maintaining Pace

- **Brief:** Managing a long stage without over-driving; recovering cleanly from a moment; preserving car and tyres across a full rally.
- **Drill:** Long stage, consistency-focused.

## S9 — Tarmac Rally

- **Brief:** Tarmac technique — more grip, a tighter line, kerb use, harder braking; the mindset shift from gravel.
- **Drill:** Tarmac stage.

## S10 — Rally Simulation + Assessment

- **Format:** Multi-stage mini-rally, combined time.
- **Assessed on:** stage times within % of benchmark, cleanliness (no major offs), note execution.
- **Outcome:** **Rally Championship-ready**; flagged for the [[racesims-the-ladder|ARKA Rally Drive]] pathway if exceptional.
