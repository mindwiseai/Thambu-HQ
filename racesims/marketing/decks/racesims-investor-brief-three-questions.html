QUESTION ONE

What is RaceSims?

RaceSims is India's integrated motorsport development platform — a place where anyone curious about motorsport can try it for the price of a movie ticket, and where those serious about it can find a real path to the racing grid through partnerships with ARKA Motorsports and Polo Cup.

The gap we bridge: Indian motorsport has thousands of aspirants and a brutally narrow entry point — kart fees, equipment costs, training infrastructure, family connections, and time gate all but a few from ever sitting in a real race car. RaceSims is the on-ramp — affordable to try, structured for serious development, with a documented path to real-world race seats at the end.

Three businesses, one brand:

  • Operator of premium sim racing centres — Chennai flagship opens 2026, with a 43-centre national rollout mapped over five years
  • Official distributor and service partner in India for Conspit and VNM — the world's leading premium sim racing hardware brands
  • Championship organiser — the National Sim Racing Championship launches 2027 with broadcast, sponsors, and real-world race seats at the end

Inside every centre sits The Motorsport Academy (structured curriculum from Initiation through Pro Track) and The Ladder (the sim-to-real pathway via ARKA Rally Drive and Polo Cup Follow Car). Founded by Thambu Samy, a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports — India's most-decorated motorsport team with 130+ national championship titles since 1987.
