02 · What is RaceSims?

India's gateway to motorsport.

Whether you want to try racing once or pursue it as a career — this is where you start. India has thousands of motorsport aspirants. Unlike most other sports, the entry barrier into motorsport is excruciatingly high. Kart fees, equipment costs, training infrastructure, time — all gate the path to a real cockpit. Everyone aspires; few get to try. RaceSims removes that gate. We make racing accessible for the price of a movie ticket, and give those serious about it a structured ladder all the way to the grid.

Built across four lines of business
01
RaceSims Experience Centres
Premium sim racing venues where anyone in India can come experience motorsport. Walk-in sessions · monthly memberships · corporate team events · private parties · café-lounge. Chennai flagship opens 2026, scaling to a 100-centre owned-and-operated national network.
02
RaceSims Hardware Distribution
Official distributor and service partner in India for world-leading premium sim racing hardware brands. Every centre doubles as a showroom — bringing world-class racing equipment to Indian consumers and businesses with full after-sales service.
03
RaceSims Motorsport Academy
Where Indians learn to race. Beginner courses · progressive curriculum (Initiation → Race Academy → Rally School → Pro Track) · Track Day Programmes for supercar owners · Real Driving Experiences with ARKA + Buzzing Hornet · 1-on-1 coaching · B2B team training.
04
RaceSims Championships
India's first national-level sim racing leagues — both circuit and rally. RaceSims Racing Championship + Rally Championship launch 2027 · broadcast · sponsored · structured seasons with prize structures. Top finishers earn real race seats through our partnerships.

Founded by Thambu Samy, working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports — India's most-decorated motorsport team with 130+ national championship titles since 1987.

10 · The operating model

All owned. All operated. No franchise leakage, no brand dilution.

Why owned beats franchised

LensFranchiseOwned
Revenue capture per centre6% royalty100%
Hardware margin (centres)~15% wholesale30-40% retail
Brand control (CX, design, ops)VariableAbsolute
Network compoundingSlowImmediate
Speed of brand-standard rolloutMonths per centreWeeks
Capex per centre0 (franchisee pays)₹95L-₹4.5cr
Y5 revenue per centre to RaceSims~₹12-15 L₹2-6 cr

Trading capital intensity for revenue capture. The full upside accrues to RaceSims and its shareholders.

Why this is the right structure

  • Brand integrity is the moat. Motorsport is unforgiving — one bad-experience franchisee can poison the brand in a city. Owned operation means every customer experience is brand-controlled.
  • Hardware distribution leverage compounds. 100 owned centres × ~₹35L/yr Y5 hardware-to-consumer = ₹35 cr revenue stream that doesn't exist in a franchise model.
  • Championship integrity. All centres run the same championship system, the same broadcast standard, the same sponsor experience — no franchise variability undermining the league.
  • Talent pipeline. RaceSims-trained centre managers become regional ops leaders → city heads → next-generation founders. A franchise model breaks that career ladder.
  • Exit optionality. A 100-owned-centre business sells at 10-15× revenue. A 100-franchise business sells at 4-6× royalty stream. Same network, 3-4× the exit value.

Capital-heavier upfront. Materially bigger Y5 outcome. Cleaner exit.
