## Overview
The defining strategic concept behind [[ierl]]: create a structured pathway for sim racing drivers to eventually compete in real motorsport events. This is the "ultimate sim-to-reality experience in India."

## Pathway
1. **Hot lap events** — monthly IERL rounds at partner sim centres
2. **Quarterly championships** — top performers compete across the network
3. **Real car experience** — the best driver eventually drives a real race car

## Why this matters
The sim-to-reality angle differentiates IERL from generic esports leagues. The exclusivity criteria (hardware spec + VMS software + vision alignment) exist specifically to ensure the network is populated by partners who understand and support this vision. When stakes are low (hot laps), uniformity matters for fairness. When stakes are high (real car prize), the vision alignment matters existentially.
