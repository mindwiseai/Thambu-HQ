06 · Driver Progression & Fair Competition

Fast drivers move up. Everyone has a chance.

Promotion Ladder

01
First visit — every new driver starts in Amateur by default. No entry barrier.
02
Voluntary upgrade — any driver can choose Pro at any time from the kiosk.
03
Win Amateur network-wide — automatically promoted to Pro the following month.
04
Finish top 3 in Amateur twice — mandatory Pro promotion. No exceptions.
05
Pro is permanent. No downgrade. No sandbagging back to Amateur for easier prizes.

Three Layers of Fair Competition

1 — Promotion system
Dominant amateur drivers graduate to Pro within 1–2 months. The amateur class stays genuinely competitive for newer players. Natural refresh every season.
2 — Local centre prize
Every centre has its own leaderboard. The fastest driver at your centre wins a local prize each month — regardless of network standings. Every centre, every month, guaranteed winner.
3 — Track rotation from Season 2
A Monza specialist won't necessarily dominate Spa. Rotating tracks every month levels the field naturally across the season.

The driver who wins every month is your best marketing asset — not a problem. "Can you beat the record?" is a more compelling reason to come back than a blank leaderboard.

07 · How It Works

Walk in. Enter your name. Set your lap. Done.

The Customer Experience

01
Kiosk screen on each rig — customer enters their name. System sets it automatically in the game.
02
Picks Amateur or Pro — game launches directly into the IERL server. No menus to navigate.
03
Sets their fastest lap — live leaderboard on screen shows where they rank across all centres.
04
Session ends — kiosk resets for the next customer. Lap time is recorded permanently for the month.

Platform Details

TRACK LIMITS Enforced server-side — cuts = invalid lap
YOUR SETUP Assetto Corsa on each rig — we handle the rest
Two servers run simultaneously — one for Amateur, one for Pro. Separate leaderboards, same track, same month. RaceSims manages all software, updates, and results.
