## Formula
**₹1,00,000 ÷ total rigs = per-rig fee**

The pool is fixed at ₹1,00,000 every month regardless of network size. Per-rig cost decreases as more centres join. This inverts the usual incentive: partners benefit when more centres join rather than feeling diluted.

Fees are rounded to clean numbers; any overage is retained in the pool.

## Prizes
Amazon Gift Vouchers. Per class (₹50K each):
- 1st: ₹30,000
- 2nd: ₹12,500
- 3rd: ₹7,500

Local prize: ₹500/rig to each centre's top qualifying driver.
