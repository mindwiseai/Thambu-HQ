# The Motorsport Academy — Delivery System

The **conduct layer** of the [[racesims-academy-curriculum|Academy]]. The curriculum page defines *what* we teach (Levels, glossary, gates); this page defines *how a coach actually runs it* — to a stopwatch, to a consistent standard, at every RaceSims location. This is the operating manual you hand a certified coach.

> Training is the centrepiece. The difference between "supervised seat time" and a programme that *makes a real difference* is three things: the data is the teacher, the driver always knows their next step, and any certified coach delivers the same quality. Everything below serves those three.

---

## The three non-negotiables

1. **Data, not vibes** — every session ends with a *number that went up*. Telemetry, not feel, is the evidence of improvement.
2. **A spine, not lessons** — the driver always knows their [[racesims-academy-curriculum|Level]], their current [[racesims-academy-challenges|gate]], and their next one. No aimless track time.
3. **A system, not a star coach** — the Coach Playbook (Layer 5) makes quality identical across the RaceSims partner network. This is what lets the Academy scale.

---

## Layer 1 — The Session Engine

The atomic unit. [[racesims-academy-curriculum|B·D·A·D]] specified to the minute, with explicit coach actions. **Hard rule: one concept per session. Never two.**

### 60-minute private session

| Beat | Min | Coach does | Driver does |
|------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| **Brief** | 8 | Open last session's data + Record. State today's ONE objective. Show the BAD vs GOOD for the target skill on the telemetry/whiteboard. | Watches the contrast, sets a target number. |
| **Drill** | 20 | Isolate the skill (e.g. braking, no apex pressure). Call the delta after each rep. | Repeats the [[racesims-drill-library|drill]] with feedback every rep. |
| **Apply** | 22 | Put the skill into full laps. Build consistency under realistic conditions. | Runs laps; coach calls live. |
| **Debrief** | 10 | Overlay start-trace vs end-trace. **Log the number to the Record.** Set homework (a solo Trainer). Name the next gate. | Sees the improvement, leaves with one task. |

### 30-minute session (compressed)
Brief 5 · Drill 15 · short runs 7 · Debrief 3. No separate Apply — the drill bleeds straight into short runs. Still ends with a logged number + one homework Trainer.

### The two rules that make it stick
- **Open on data, close on data.** Never start without last session's trace; never end without today's logged.
- **One homework Trainer, always.** The driver leaves with exactly one [[racesims-academy-challenges|Practice Trainer]] to beat before next time. That's the between-session loop.

## Layer 2 — The Drill Library

The catalogue of named, scored exercises that every session is built from. Each concept in the [[racesims-academy-curriculum|glossary]] maps to a drill with a setup, a telemetry metric, and bronze/silver/gold marks. Full catalogue: **[[racesims-drill-library]]**.

The coached drills *are* the self-serve [[racesims-academy-challenges|Practice Trainers]] — identical vocabulary, so a coached session and solo homework reinforce the same motor pattern.

---

## Layer 3 — The Workshop Format (groups & events)

The group/event delivery for the [[racesims-master-rate-card|8-seat]] room. A themed **2–3 hour workshop**:

| Block | Time | What happens |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Theatre** | 30 min | Classroom: the theme concept, BAD vs GOOD, a pro onboard breakdown. Everyone learns the same idea. |
| **Stations** | 90 min | Drivers rotate through [[racesims-drill-library|drill]] rigs. Coach floats. Live leaderboard on the lounge screen. |
| **Race** | 45 min | Apply the theme under pressure — a mini-[[racesims-championship-architecture|championship]] heat with [[racesims-curriculum-racecraft|racecraft]]. |
| **Podium Debrief** | 15 min | Telemetry review, awards, next-step nudge. |

**Themed editions** (each sellable + a monthly retention hook): *Brake Later · Race Starts & Turn 1 · Wet Weather · Qualifying Lap · Wheel-to-Wheel.* Run one a month and the room has a reason to come back that isn't a private booking.

---

## Layer 4 — The Driver Development Record

Every driver has a living profile on [[racesims-vms-build|VMS]]:

- Current **Level** + progress to the next gate
- **Drill PBs** — bronze/silver/gold against every Trainer attempted
- **Session notes** + the full **telemetry history**
- **Gate results** — passed / repeating

This is what makes progress *compound* across visits — and what makes RaceSims sticky. Your record lives with us; leaving means starting the evidence over somewhere with none.

---

## Layer 5 — The Coach Playbook

The scaling layer — turns "good coaching" into a repeatable system.

- **Certification** — coaches are trained and signed off to deliver the B·D·A·D format and the drill library to standard before they run paying sessions.
- **Per-drill cues** — each [[racesims-drill-library|drill]] card carries: what to *say*, what to *read in the trace*, and the *common error* to catch. A new coach runs the same session a veteran does.
- **The quality bar** (audit-able): *every session ends with a logged number; every driver leaves knowing their next gate.* If either is missing, the session wasn't delivered.

---

## The retention loop (why it commercially works)

```
TELEMETRY → DRILL → GATE → RECORD → CHAMPIONSHIP
   (a number)  (a task)  (a test)  (it remembers)  (a reason)
```

A driver always has a number that moved today, a drill to practise, a gate to clear, a record that remembers, and a championship to climb toward. Each arrow is a reason to book again.
