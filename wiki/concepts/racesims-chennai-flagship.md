# RaceSims Chennai Flagship Experience Centre

The direct-operated premium sim racing centre opening in Chennai under [[racesims-company|RaceSims Solutions Pvt Ltd]]. **Total investment ₹66 lakhs**, monthly opex ₹4.97L, Year 1 EBITDA projection ₹32L, full payback ~17 months. Serves as the proof point and franchise prototype for the [[racesims-india-saturation-plan|43-centre India saturation plan]]. Planning conversation captured in [[2026-05-17-racesims-chennai-flagship-planning]].

## What this is

A 1,800-2,000 sqft premium sim racing venue with 6 rigs, café, briefing room, and spectator capacity. Not a gaming café — a professional motorsport development facility positioned via [[racesims-drive-train-compete|Drive · Train · Compete]] with a clear pathway from walk-in customer to real-motorsport seat via [[racesims-the-ladder|the Ladder]].

## Floor space and layout

- **Built-up: 1,800-2,000 sqft.** Earlier 1,500 sqft estimate insufficient for proper spectator capacity during Live Race events.
- **Zones**: 6 rig bays (680 sqft, 2 rows of 3) + café/lounge (270 sqft, doubles as spectator zone) + briefing room (170 sqft) + reception (150 sqft) + storage/server (110 sqft) + 2 restrooms + circulation
- **Target areas**: Anna Nagar (₹85/sqft), Kilpauk (₹75/sqft — best size-for-money), Alwarpet (₹95/sqft). Nungambakkam over budget for 1,800 sqft.

## Strategic role

This is **not just a venue.** Per the planning conversation, Chennai serves three purposes:

1. **Operating business** — generates ₹32L EBITDA in Y1, ₹87L in Y2
2. **Franchise prototype** — every process documented as the playbook for the [[racesims-india-saturation-plan|43-centre network]]
3. **Brand proof point** — first place in India where sim racing is positioned as motorsport infrastructure, not gaming entertainment
