# Chennai Flagship Proposal v2 — Investor-grade Business Case

11-slide single-centre proposal built bottom-up over a long session: rig spec (Conspit/VNM at distributor cost), capex line-by-line, staffing rebuilt 4 times to land on the lean model, opex scaled defensibly, base-case utilisation respecting the capacity ceiling, ancillary decomposed and merch recalibrated.

## Summary

Locked the **₹89L Y1 single-centre business case** for Chennai flagship — replaces earlier ₹66-68L planning estimate from May 17 and supersedes own earlier v2 (₹92L). Honest rebuild over deep-dive session using Excel rig specs (5 Rally + 1 Motion at Conspit/VNM cost), full infrastructure breakdown, lean 4-person team, base-case utilisation ramp respecting the 26,280-hour annual ceiling, recalibrated merch attach, line-by-line overhead audit (rent corrected to ₹1.25L for found 2,000 sq ft place, electricity honest recalc to ₹50K with 5-star ACs, maintenance trimmed to ₹7.5K, payment gateway as 2% of digital revenue, music licence removed, insurance properly sized for ₹50L+ asset cover). **5-year cumulative EBITDA ₹5.24cr, 24-month payback (end of Y2), 5.9× capital multiple, 70% Y5 EBITDA margin, 21% blended break-even.** Brand architecture locked: centre = "RaceSims · Chennai" (low-friction door), [[racesims-motorsport-academy|The Motorsport Academy]] is the programme inside.

## Key facts

### Capex — ₹92L total
- **Rigs ₹39.09L** — 5 Rally Rig @ ₹5,84,900 each + 1 Motion Rig @ ₹9,84,900 (Conspit/VNM at our distributor cost)
- **Infrastructure ₹27.11L** — space prep (HVAC, interior, flooring, lighting, furniture) ₹8.5L, power/UPS ₹2.5L, IT/software/networking ₹2.55L, brand/CX/café/event-kit ₹4.6L, safety/spares ₹1.08L, deposits/pre-opening ₹7.88L (incl. 6 mo rent deposit @ ₹1.5L = ₹9L)
- **Hard capex ₹69.32L**
- **Working capital ₹23.13L** (5 months × ₹4.63L)
- **Grand total ₹92.45L**
- **Built-in equity** — rigs deployed at ₹39L = ₹65-70L retail. Day-one balance-sheet equity ₹26-31L

### Distributor edge
- We build a ₹65L centre for ₹39L — every centre
- Structural moat against any competitor: ₹26-31L cost advantage per centre
- The single biggest reason multi-centre rollout works

### Monthly opex — ₹4.63L (annual ₹55.5L Y1)

**Staffing (final lean model, v5 after 5 iterations):**
- Senior Team Lead ₹35K (multi-role: floor + sim ops + marketing + Zoho Books)
- 2 × Multi-role Team Members @ ₹20K each
- Housekeeping ₹5K
- Statutory loading 28% (PF + ESI + bonus)
- Outsourced: CA filings ₹7K. **No security guard, no in-house accountant, no marketing-only hire.**
- Total staff cost: ₹1.09L/month

**Overheads:**
- Rent ₹1.5L (premium location)
- Electricity ₹1.05L
- Maintenance ₹25K, Marketing ₹25K, Incentive pool reserve ₹25K
- Software, insurance, bank, music, misc ₹23.2K

### Revenue model — base case
- **Capacity ceiling: 26,280 seat-hours/year** (6 rigs × 12 hr × 365 days) — immovable
- **Utilisation ramp:** 30% → 50% → 60% → 65% → 68% (revised down from 72% Y5 aggressive to base case)
- **Blended rate ramp:** ₹750 → ₹800 → ₹900 → ₹1,000 → ₹1,100/hr
- **Y1 break-even at 28% util** — Y1 lands at 30%, just above
- Industry benchmark for mature entertainment venues: 50-65%. **68% Y5 is defensible-ambitious.**

### Ancillary revenue (recalibrated)
- **Grows from 14% → 28% of rig revenue** (revised down from 15→30% after merch correction)
- **Café & F&B** ~50% of ancillary (60-65% attach rate, ₹150-250 avg ticket)
- **Events & corporate** ~25% (corporate ₹40-60K, birthdays ₹15-25K, private nights ₹30-50K) — conservative line, real upside
- **Coaching & academy** ~8% (₹2,500-3,500 1-on-1, group masterclasses, multi-week courses with pro driver tie-ups)
- **Championship entries** ~5% (from 2027, ₹2,500-5,000 entry, season passes ₹15-25K)
- **Merchandise** ~6-9% (recalibrated from 12-15%) — realistic 3-4% walk-in attach + event-day spikes. No daily 10% attach assumption.

### Peak/off-peak saturation reality (Y5)
- Weekday off-peak (11am-6pm, 210 hrs/wk): ~45% util — room to grow
- Weekday peak (6pm-11pm, 150 hrs/wk): ~90% util — near full
- Weekend (12 hrs × 2 = 144 hrs/wk): ~88% util — near full
- **Centre #2 trigger: weekday peak hits 90%+ for 3 consecutive months** (expected mid-Y3)

## Decisions locked
1. **6-rig mix: 5 Rally + 1 Motion** (per Thambu instruction, from Excel: RaceSims Outlet.xlsx)
2. **Rig specs sourced from Excel** — Conspit/VNM components at distributor cost. Rally ₹5,84,900, Motion ₹9,84,900
3. **Infrastructure rebuilt from scratch** — no Excel bundling. Each line itemised with reasoning.
4. **Lean 4-person team** — Senior Lead + 2 multi-role + housekeeping. CA outsourced for filings. No security (maintenance bumped ₹10K to compensate). No in-house accountant/marketing (HQ does not absorb their cost into this centre P&L because they don't exist as separate roles).
5. **Rent ₹1.5L** (premium location), 6-month deposit
6. **Café handled by team** in v1 — partner brand (Blue Tokai/Subko/Third Wave) deferred for Y2 if quality requires
7. **Base case utilisation** — 30/50/60/65/68 (not aggressive 30/50/65/70/72). Defensible to investors.
8. **Working capital 5 months** of opex
9. **Merch attach 3-4%** walk-in (not 10%) + event-day spikes — realistic for sim racing centre

## Open questions
- **Café operating model** — boys train on espresso vs. partner brand kiosk. Defer to Y2 decision once we see attach data.
- **Incentive plan design** — pool reserved at ₹25K/month; structure (revenue share %, event bonuses, content KPIs, tenure bonus) to be designed later.
- **Manager scaling trigger** — when do we add a 3rd multi-role member? Suggested: when util crosses 50% sustainably (Y2).
- **Motion rig pricing** — locked at ₹1,200-1,500 off-peak / ₹1,800-2,500 peak. Validate against early customer willingness-to-pay.
