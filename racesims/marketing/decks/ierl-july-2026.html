06 · Driver Classes & Fair Competition

Fast drivers move up. Everyone has a real shot.

Promotion Ladder

1
Default — Amateur
Every new driver starts in Amateur. No barrier to entry. Walk in and race.
2
Voluntary Pro upgrade
Any driver can choose to race the Pro class anytime they want.
3
Win Amateur network-wide
Automatic promotion to Pro the following month.
4
Top 3 in Amateur — twice
Mandatory promotion. No exceptions. Protects the Amateur field.
5
Pro is permanent
No downgrade path. No sandbagging back to Amateur for easier wins.

Three Layers of Fair Competition

1 — Promotion system keeps Amateur fresh
Dominant drivers graduate within 1–2 months. New customers always have a genuine shot at the Amateur leaderboard. No one dominates for long.
2 — Local centre champion, every month
Regardless of network ranking, every centre crowns its fastest driver. A customer who'll never be #1 nationally can still be the champion of your city. That's a reason to come back.
3 — Track rotation from Season 2
A specialist in one track won't necessarily dominate the next. Rotating combos every month naturally levels the field. No single driver can grind indefinitely.

The driver who keeps winning is your best marketing asset, not a problem. "Can you beat the record at [your centre]?" is the most compelling reason for a customer to book again.

Why centre-only Every rig runs identical hardware and the Real Penalty enforcement software — strict track limits, automatic penalties, no modified files. A fully controlled environment with zero scope for cheating. That's why IERL runs in-centre, never from home.

09 · Payment Flow — Direct Settlement

No central pot. Money moves like Splitwise.

The league never holds your money. At month-end it calculates who pays whom — matching each dealer's contribution straight to winners — so every payout is peer-to-peer and each dealer makes just one or two UPI transfers.

How the settlement works

1
Aug 1 — Leaderboard locked
Top 3 per class confirmed. Prize amounts known.
2
The league runs the settlement
Each dealer's contribution is matched to specific winners, minimising the number of transfers — Splitwise-style.
3
Each dealer gets one simple instruction
"Pay ₹X to [winner's UPI]." Usually one transfer, sometimes two.
4
Dealers pay directly by Aug 5
Screenshot in the group. The league verifies. Month closed.
Why this matters: the league never touches a rupee — every payout is dealer → winner. No middleman holding the pot, no "only one party handles the money." The full settlement sheet is shared with everyone. Total transparency.
