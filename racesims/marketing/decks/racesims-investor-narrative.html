What is RaceSims?

Indian motorsport is in a moment most of us have waited a long time for. F1 viewership is growing every year, the INRC is delivering season after season of close racing, and teams like ARKA Motorsports have spent four decades building championship pedigree that quietly rivals anything in Asia. The audience is there. The talent is there. What's been narrow is the runway in between — the path from loving racing to racing.

That's the gap RaceSims is built to fill.

We are India's integrated motorsport development platform — a place where someone who loves racing can develop the craft, compete in real championships, and earn a path toward real-world seats through partners like ARKA and Polo Cup. We're not replacing the motorsport ecosystem. We're adding a new on-ramp to it.

Three things make this possible now

01 · The equipment
Direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, and load-cell pedals — the same technology behind F1 driver simulators — has finally become affordable enough to deploy commercially in India.
02 · The pedigree
As a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports (130+ national championship titles since 1987), every curriculum, every coaching session, every centre standard is shaped by people who have stood on real race grids. RaceSims is sim racing built by motorsport people. That's also what makes our pathway real — top Motorsport Academy graduates earn real-world seat opportunities through our partnerships with ARKA and the Polo Cup ecosystem.
03 · The integration
RaceSims is a direct distributor in India for both Conspit and VNM — the world's leading premium sim racing brands. Every centre we build is at trade pricing — a ₹65L spec for ₹39L of capital. But the deeper edge isn't the hardware cost — it's that we operate the full development stack under one brand: centres + hardware + championship + curriculum + sim-to-real pathway. Everyone else in this market plays one part of that. We play all of them.

RaceSims is three businesses under one brand. We operate premium sim racing centres — the flagship opens in Chennai in 2026, with a 43-centre national rollout mapped over five years. We distribute Conspit and VNM hardware nationally. And we organise the National Sim Racing Championship — launching in 2027, with broadcast, sponsors, and a real seat at the end for the season's best.

Underneath sits The Motorsport Academy — the structured curriculum from Initiation through Race Academy, Rally School, and Pro Track. And The Ladder — the sim-to-seat pathway that gives our best graduates a real shot at being India's next racer.

Where I see RaceSims in three years

There's a particular kind of dream that nearly every Indian teenager has held at some point — racing driver. It's one of the most enduring aspirations of growing up in this country, fed by F1 broadcasts on Sunday afternoons, the INRC at Coimbatore, MotoGP reruns, Drive to Survive on Netflix. The audience for motorsport in India is in the millions and growing 25%+ a year. The dream is common.

What's been missing is a place to develop it. We are building that place.

By May 2029, RaceSims will be where thousands of Indian teenagers count their racing journey as having begun. A 15-year-old who watched a Polo Cup round on YouTube in 2026 and walked into our Chennai centre will, three years later, be qualifying for their first INRC round. A young woman in Hyderabad who never thought motorsport could include her will be on our championship grid. A teenager in Vijayawada whose family could never have afforded karting will be racing — because the path finally had a starting point they could reach.

The moats that hold it together

The pedigree moat — ARKA exclusivity papered, first Academy graduates in ARKA cars, real seats earned by real drivers. Years to build, almost impossible to replicate.

The integration moat — the only brand in India running centres + championship + curriculum + sim-to-seat pathway under one roof. Others compete with one slice. We hold the whole.

The cost moat — direct Conspit and VNM distribution. Every centre built at trade pricing. The national network funds itself off operating cash from Year 3 onward.

The network moat — 17 centres feeding into one another's leaderboards, championship rounds, and community. The brand becomes a flywheel, not a venue chain.

The brand moat — "Motorsport Academy graduate" becomes a recognised credential inside the Indian motorsport world. That language takes years to earn and is permanent once it lands.
