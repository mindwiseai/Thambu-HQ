# RaceSims Revenue Stack — Six Strategic Adjacencies

Six additional revenue streams beyond the base [[racesims-chennai-flagship|centre operations]] + franchise + championship, layered onto the same infrastructure to take Year 5 revenue from ₹83 cr → ₹99 cr and valuation from ₹1,000-1,300 cr → ₹1,250-1,650 cr. Detailed in [[2026-05-17-racesims-chennai-flagship-planning|the flagship planning session]] May 17.

## The six streams (combined)

| Stream | Y5 Revenue | Y5 EBITDA | Capex Required |
|--------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| AMC | ₹1.19 cr | ₹78 L | ₹37 L |
| Junior Motorsport Academy | ₹2.73 cr | ₹2.17 cr | ₹14 L |
| Merch | ₹4.16 cr | ₹1.45 cr | ₹75 L |
| Coaching & Engineering Academy | ₹86.3 L | ₹41 L | ₹31 L |
| Karting Academy | ₹6.32 cr | ₹2.40 cr | ₹7.40 cr |
| College Curriculum | ₹93.5 L | ₹59.5 L | ₹19 L |
| **Total** | **₹16.2 cr** | **₹7.81 cr** | **₹8.76 cr** |

Karting Academy is the single largest capex (₹7.40 cr) and largest revenue contributor of the six — driven by 2 owned karting venues built in Years 4-5.

## 1. AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)

**Hardware servicing business** for RaceSims rigs at franchise centres, consumer homes, and third-party sim cafes.

### Tiers
| Tier | Customer | Annual Fee |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Network AMC (mandatory for franchises) | All franchise rigs | ₹15,000/rig |
| Premium Home AMC | High-end home rig owners | ₹12,000/rig |
| Standard Home Service | Regular home rig owners | ₹6,000/rig |
| On-demand Repair | Any rig owner | ₹3,500/call |

### Y5 economics
- 186 franchise rigs × ₹15K = ₹27.9 L
- 300 Premium Home × ₹12K = ₹36 L
- 500 Standard Home × ₹6K = ₹30 L
- 300 on-demand calls × ₹3.5K = ₹10.5 L
- Upgrade revenue through visits: ₹15 L
- **Total: ₹1.19 cr revenue · ₹78L EBITDA (65% margin)**

### Operations
- 4 regional service teams (South/North/West/East) — 1 technician + 1 helper each
- ₹41L annual cost (teams, vehicles, parts, dispatch)
- Strategic value: regular customer touchpoints → upgrade opportunities + reliability moat

## 2. Junior Motorsport Academy

**Kids 8-17 programme** that builds 10-year customer LTV and feeds [[racesims-the-ladder|the Ladder]] talent pipeline. Run only at [[racesims-india-saturation-plan|Mega Flagships]].

### Programmes
| Programme | Age | Format | Annual Fee |
|-----------|-----|--------|-----------|
| Cadets | 8-11 | 1 session/week, 90 min | ₹50,000 |
| Juniors | 12-14 | 2 sessions/week + monthly karting | ₹70,000 |
| Cadets Pro | 15-17 | 3+ sessions + karting league + ARKA observation | ₹1,00,000 |

### Y5 economics (60 students per Mega × 7 Megas = 420 students)
- 210 Cadets × ₹50K = ₹1.05 cr
- 140 Juniors × ₹70K = ₹98 L
- 70 Cadets Pro × ₹1L = ₹70 L
- **Total: ₹2.73 cr revenue · ₹2.17 cr EBITDA (80% margin)**

### Strategic value
**Highest LTV adjacency.** A 10-year-old at Cadets → 14-year-old at Juniors → 17-year-old at Cadets Pro → 18-year-old auto-converting to Championship Pass with 50% discount → corporate customer at 25 → home rig buyer for life. Per-graduate LTV: ₹3-5 lakhs over 15 years.

Plus parents become customers themselves (corporate connections).

## 3. Merchandise

**Branded apparel + lifestyle + premium collectibles + functional racing accessories.**

### Product line
- Core apparel: t-shirts (₹800-1,500), polos (₹1,500-2,500), hoodies (₹2,500-4,000), jackets (₹4,000-8,000), caps (₹600-1,200)
- Lifestyle: backpacks, water bottles, stationery, keychains
- **Premium / Limited Edition**: Championship Winner Editions, ARKA × RaceSims collaborations, signed Real-Drive Winner items
- Functional: gloves (partner brands), helmet stickers, racing apparel

### Y5 economics
- 7 Mega Flagship retail corners: ₹1.51 cr (1,200 trans/year each × ₹1,800 avg)
- 36 Boutique merch corners (smaller): ₹1.30 cr
- Online D2C: ₹80 L
- Event drops: ₹25 L
- Wholesale/B2B: ₹30 L
- **Total: ₹4.16 cr revenue · ₹1.45 cr EBITDA (35% margin)**

### Capex
₹75 L total: design, initial inventory (₹35L), retail fixtures across 7 Megas (₹21L), e-commerce platform (₹5L).

### Strategic value
Walking advertising — every customer wearing RaceSims is 30 impressions of marketing. Plus collectibles drive event attendance + future export potential.

## 4. Coaching & Engineering Academy

**Two parallel certification tracks** — creating a national pool of certified sim coaches and motorsport engineers. Positioned as "India's first professional sim racing and motorsport engineering certification programme" — FIA-equivalent for sim coaching.

### Track A: RaceSims Certified Driver Coach (CDC)
- 4-week intensive at Chennai HQ
- 3 cohorts/year × 10 candidates = 30/year
- ₹75,000 per person → ₹22.5 L annual

### Track B: RaceSims Certified Tuner Engineer (CTE)
- 6-week programme at Chennai HQ
- 3 cohorts/year × 8 candidates = 24/year
- ₹1,20,000 per person → ₹28.8 L annual

### Track C: Online self-paced versions
- Online CDC ₹25,000 (60 enrollments/year) → ₹15 L
- Online CTE ₹35,000 (40 enrollments/year) → ₹14 L

### Plus recertification: ₹6 L/year

### Y5 total: ₹86.3 L revenue · ₹41 L EBITDA (47% margin)

### Strategic value
- **Talent pipeline** — every franchise needs head coaches + head engineers; your certified pool fills these roles
- **Quality control** across the network
- **B2B credentialing** — race teams, OEMs hire your certified people
- **Authority positioning** — "Certified by RaceSims" becomes the standard

## 5. Karting Academy

**The most strategically valuable of the six.** Closes the complete pathway: Sim → Karting → Championship → ARKA/Polo Cup. **No competitor in India can offer this full vertical.**

### Two-stage build

**Stage 1 (Year 3-4): Partner Karting Programme**
- Partner with existing karting tracks in 4 cities (Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi)
- RaceSims provides curriculum + structure + sim integration
- Track provides infrastructure + karts
- 50-50 revenue split

**Stage 2 (Year 4-5): Own Karting Centres**
- Build 2 RaceSims-branded karting centres (Chennai + Bangalore)
- 500-800m track + facility
- ₹3.5 cr investment per venue

### Programmes
| Programme | Age | Annual Fee |
|-----------|-----|-----------|
| Foundation Karting | 12-14 | ₹35,000 |
| Advanced Karting | 14-17 | ₹70,000 |
| Premium Karting Pathway | 15-17 | ₹1,40,000 |
| Adult Karting Programme | 18+ | ₹40,000 |

### Y5 economics
- Partner programmes (4 cities, 200 students): ₹1.27 cr revenue (₹63.5 L RaceSims share)
- Own karting venues (2 × ₹2.59 cr): ₹5.18 cr revenue
- RaceSims Karting Series sponsorship: ₹50 L
- **Total: ₹6.32 cr revenue · ₹2.40 cr EBITDA (38% margin)**

### Capex
₹7.40 cr total (₹15 L partner launch + ₹3.5 cr × 2 own venues + ₹25 L race series infra).

### Strategic value
- **The Pathway becomes complete** — Sim → Karting → Championship → Real Drive under one brand
- **Talent identification** for ARKA's sim-to-rally programme
- **Junior Academy graduation pathway** — retains 14-year-olds for 6 more years
- **Premium pricing legitimacy** — having real karts strengthens premium pricing on everything else

## 6. College Curriculum

**License RaceSims curriculum to engineering colleges + motorsport programmes + sports management schools.** Lowest revenue, highest strategic value for long-term talent and credibility.

### Four product types
| Type | Customer | Annual Fee |
|------|----------|-----------|
| A — Motorsport Engineering Elective | Engineering colleges (8 partners) | ₹4 L/semester × 8 = ₹32 L |
| B — BAJA/SUPRA/FS team support | College racing teams (15 partners) | ₹2.5 L × 15 = ₹37.5 L |
| C — Hospitality schools case study | Tourism/hospitality schools (5 partners) | ₹2 L × 5 = ₹10 L |
| D — Sports management module | Sports management institutes (5) | ₹2 L × 5 = ₹10 L |

Plus guest lecture circuit: ₹4 L/year

### Y5 total: ₹93.5 L revenue · ₹59.5 L EBITDA (64% margin)

### Strategic value
- **Talent pipeline** — interns become full-time hires
- **Academic credibility** — "RaceSims curriculum used at IIT Madras"-type credentials elevate brand
- **BAJA/SUPRA engineering teams** — next-generation motorsport engineers come through your programme
- **Future AICTE/UGC accreditation potential** — long-term curriculum recognition

## Combined impact on the master plan

**Without these 6 streams (base [[racesims-india-saturation-plan|India saturation]]):**
- Y5 Revenue: ₹83 cr
- Y5 EBITDA: ₹33 cr
- Y5 Valuation: ₹1,000-1,300 cr

**With these 6 streams added:**
- Y5 Revenue: **₹99 cr** (+19%)
- Y5 EBITDA: **₹41 cr** (+24%)
- Y5 Valuation: **₹1,250-1,650 cr** (+25%)

Additional capital: ₹8.76 cr (almost entirely in Years 3-5, funded from operating cash flow by then).

```
Junior Academy ────► feeds ────► Karting Academy
       │                                │
       └──► Championship Pass at 18+ ◄──┘
                       │
                       ▼
              [[racesims-the-ladder|Real Drive]]

Coaching/Engineering Academy ──► supplies coaches/engineers ──► All centres
                              ──► supplies talent ──► B2B services

College Curriculum ──► creates BAJA partnerships ──► OEM relationships

AMC ──► retains hardware customers ──► more hardware sales ──► more network growth
```

Together these turn RaceSims from "a sim racing centre chain" into **"India's national motorsport development institution"** — and that's the brand worth ₹1,500+ cr.
