How it works

One hot lap. One month. One winner.

Each month, every IERL partner runs the same car around the same circuit. Drivers post their fastest lap to a public time-trial board. The clock decides everything. We pull every result, dedupe, and publish a single national leaderboard — with championship points scored across the season.

The Indian Esports Racing League is presented by RaceSims, India's pro-grade sim-racing brand. Data refreshes hourly from partner timing pages.
