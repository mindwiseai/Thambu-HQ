About this catalogue

The complete RaceSims product line, organised the way a race engineer thinks about a build: controls first, cockpit second, displays third, audio + haptics fourth, accessories last. Pre-configured presets and a configurator walk customers from "I want a sim" to a quotable price in three decisions.

"Most sim-racing retailers sell components. RaceSims sells systems. Every kit is built around the principle that the driver's connection to the car is in the controls and the seat — and everything else exists to support that connection."

Three principles that show up everywhere in this catalogue

  1. The wheelbase is the spine of the build. Pick this first; everything else slots in to match its capability.
  2. The pedals are where most lap time lives. Cheap pedals undercut expensive wheelbases. We don't sell mismatched stacks.
  3. Audio + haptics is not an afterthought. It's a separate tier with its own decisions.

08Commercial / B2B

The consumer catalogue covers home and individual customers. For commercial — corporate experience installations, hospitality, lounges, retail experience zones, racing academies, esports content studios — RaceSims has a separate quote process and product roadmap.

The Enclosed Rig — in R&D

A fully-enclosed driving simulator cabin, RaceSims-designed, for commercial environments where customers sit inside a cabin, not on a rig. Available for early-customer development partnerships and pilots.

What the enclosed rig adds vs the consumer Pro preset

  • Full enclosure (sides, roof, customisable cladding panels)
  • Custom-branded livery surfaces (front + sides + interior)
  • Acoustic isolation — Atmos audio inside the cabin, minimal leakage outside
  • Integrated lighting (cabin ambient, idle-mode brand visuals, in-sim displays)
  • Single-cable customer-facing operation (kiosk mode, no staff intervention required)
  • Optional in-cabin webcam for guest reaction capture / social shareables
  • 24/7 commercial-grade thermal management (PC + audio + motion all rated for sustained operation)

Reference order of magnitude: ~₹35–50L+ depending on enclosure scope, cladding, motion configuration. Custom quote per project.

How to engage

This is not a configurator decision. Commercial customers should:

  1. Email Thambu at thambu@racesims.in with: use case, location, brand, target installation date, expected daily traffic / utilisation
  2. Initial discovery call to understand requirements
  3. Site survey (in-person or detailed photo/measurement walkthrough)
  4. Custom proposal with options (enclosed rig pre-prod / Thar variant / open Pro preset with cladding)
  5. Build, install, commissioning

Lead times: custom quote discovery → 4–6 weeks. Build + install → 8–12 weeks once spec is locked.
