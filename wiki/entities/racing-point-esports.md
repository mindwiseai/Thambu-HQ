## Overview
Racing Point Esports (RPE) is a sim racing centre based in Hyderabad, India. Run by Uday (see [[uday]]). Joined the [[ierl]] network in July 2026 as the largest single contributor by rig count.

**Location:** Hyderabad
**Owner/Operator:** [[uday]]
**Rigs:** 10 (as of July 2026)

## IERL role
- 10 rigs in the July 2026 network → contributes ₹37,040 to the pool (10 × ₹3,704)
- Local prize: ₹5,000 (10 rigs × ₹500/rig)
- [[uday]] also leads technical execution of the hot lap event and VMS software
