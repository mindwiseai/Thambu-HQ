# Local Manufacturing Positioning

[[racesims-company|RaceSims]] USP: the ability to fabricate, integrate, and deliver sim-racing hardware locally in Chennai rather than purely importing. This separates RaceSims from resellers who ship pre-assembled foreign kits.

## What it covers

- Rig assembly and custom cockpit builds
- B2B custom simulator installs (e.g. [[online-instruments]] Thar-based simulator, [[araya-airport]] lounge units)
- Accessory long-tail via [[in-house-3d-printing]] — extends local manufacturing from rigs/enclosures to branded accessories no importer will customise

## Why it matters

- Enables rapid pivots on custom B2B jobs (the [[online-instruments]] Thar pivot only works because RaceSims can fabricate locally)
- Compresses R&D iteration time on new components
- Supports bundled branded accessories with every kit sale
