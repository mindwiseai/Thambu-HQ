05 · Rules & Monthly Timeline

Clean. Simple. Self-enforcing.

Month End
Leaderboard locks at 11:59 PM. No more laps counted.
1st of Month
RaceSims posts final standings + winner UPI IDs in the WhatsApp group.
By 5th
Each centre transfers directly to assigned winner(s) + IRC. Screenshot posted in group.
6th
RaceSims confirms all transfers received. Publishes confirmation. Month closed.
7th Onwards
New month leaderboard is live. Fastest lap wins again.
⚠ Miss the 5th deadline = your rigs are excluded from the next month's leaderboard. No exceptions. No extensions.
