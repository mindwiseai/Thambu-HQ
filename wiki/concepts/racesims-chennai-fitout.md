The **living, sourced fit-out schedule** for [[racesims-chennai-premium-baseline|RaceSims · Chennai]]. Re-grounded line-by-line from real 2026 India prices and optimised with [[thambu]] on 2026-05-30 — see [[2026-05-30-chennai-fitout-optimisation]]. ## Deferred / off-balance-sheet (not in the ₹14.2L)
- **Phase 2 (post-break-even):** acoustic panels (₹60k–1L, judge once the room's echo is audible) · aux screen · ambient-audio soundbar.
- **Sponsor-funded:** leaderboard TV (Samsung) · interior brand-wall graphics + motorsport feature wall (Corsair/Zotac/AMD) · possibly AC (brand partner). See [[racesims-chennai-partnerships]].

## Why ₹14.2L is the right number (founder logic)
- Old ₹16.1L = **₹1,240/sqft** = bottom-of-mid, a *lean* finish, not premium. Honest premium (sourced) = **₹21.3L** (₹1,650/sqft). We optimised the honest number *down* to ₹14.2L by value-engineering + phasing + sponsor-shift, protecting the customer-facing premium surfaces (lighting, flooring, glass, AC, reception).
- Old estimate was also *incomplete* — buried the UPS and skipped WiFi. ₹14.2L is lower **and** complete.
- Clean savings that cost nothing: mid-tier lighting (−₹76k) · black-framed glass (−₹47k) · carpet vs hard floor (−₹1L) · 4-cam CCTV · attendance-only access · de-spec'd networking.
