# ₹25 Lakh Annual Marketing Budget

Annual marketing budget for [[domains/mindwise/_index|Mindwise]] confirmed by [[thambu]] on **February 12, 2026** during the Content Strategy meeting with [[sorted-agency]].

## Key Facts

- **Amount:** ₹25,00,000 (₹25 lakh) annually
- **Announced:** February 12, 2026
- **Meeting:** Content Strategy presentation ([[aarti-samant]] and Shipra)
- **Confirmed by:** [[thambu]]
- **Scope:** External marketing agency partnership + paid ads

## Context

During Meeting 5 (Content Strategy), [[thambu]] articulated the budget breakdown and long-term vision:

### Immediate Plan
- **Agency partnership:** Target ₹25L annual budget with external marketing agency
- **Focus:** Content creation, influencer partnerships, paid ads execution
- **Timeline:** First 6 months = experimentation to gather data

### Long-Term Vision
- **In-house team:** Creator + Copywriter + Designer
- **Rationale:** Build internal capability after proving market fit
- **Transition:** Agency → hybrid → in-house

## Budget Allocation Mentioned

Based on WhatsApp March 10 regroup meeting:
- **Performance marketing agency retainer:** ₹70K–1L/month
- **Paid ads budget:** ₹50K–2L/month
- **Annual total aligns with ₹25L target**
