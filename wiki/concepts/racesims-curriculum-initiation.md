# Curriculum — Initiation

**Tier 1 · 4 sessions × 1 hr · ₹8,000 · cohorts of 4–6**

Takes a complete beginner to a competent, licence-ready driver. The entry point to [[racesims-the-ladder|The Ladder]] — completing it earns the **Initiation Licence** (lifetime, unlocks Championship eligibility and the Advanced tier). Runs the [[racesims-academy-curriculum|APEX session model]].

> Outcome promise: *"In four hours, you go from never having driven a real sim to holding a licence and a logged lap time you're proud of."*

---

## Session 1 — Cockpit & Car Control

| Beat | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Brief** | The rig is not a video game. Seating + FOV, the direct-drive wheel's force feedback, the load-cell brake (you push *force*, not distance). Intro to **weight transfer** — the car has balance; inputs must be smooth. |
| **Drill** | (a) Brake modulation, no steering — feel the load cell, stop in the box. (b) Slow slalom — smooth steering, no jerk. |
| **Apply** | 10 laps of a simple circuit at 70% pace. Goal: clean, no spins. |
| **Debrief** | Input-smoothness trace. Jagged vs smooth. |

**Target:** complete clean laps unassisted.

---

## Session 2 — The Racing Line

| Beat | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Brief** | The **racing line**: out–in–out. Turn-in, **apex**, exit. Corner types (hairpin / medium / sweeper) and how the apex moves — **geometric vs late apex**. **Slow in, fast out** — sacrifice entry to maximise exit before a straight. |
| **Drill** | One corner, repeated. Hit the apex cone, unwind to a wide exit. |
| **Apply** | Full lap, line-focused. |
| **Debrief** | Line overlay vs ideal. Where the line breaks down. |

**Target:** consistent line within benchmark + 4s.

---

## Session 3 — Braking

| Beat | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Brief** | **Braking points & reference markers** (boards, kerbs). **Threshold braking** — maximum deceleration just short of lock; the load cell makes this teachable. Brake-in-a-straight-line, then intro to braking while beginning to turn. |
| **Drill** | Braking zone: brake at the board, stop in the box. Then brake-and-turn into a corner. |
| **Apply** | Full lap with proper braking markers. |
| **Debrief** | Brake + speed trace. Lock-ups, early/late braking. |

**Target:** no lock-ups; repeatable braking points.

---

## Session 4 — The Complete Lap + Assessment

| Beat | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Brief** | Linking corners into rhythm. **Vision** — look where you want to go, not at the nose. Consistency beats heroics. |
| **Drill** | Warm-up laps building pace. |
| **Assessment** | Timed run — must beat the **Licence Lap** benchmark. Cohort race to finish. |
| **Debrief** | Full-lap review; Driver Card created on [[racesims-vms-build|VMS]]. |

**Outcomes:**
- **Pass** → beat benchmark → **Initiation Licence** + certificate
- **Distinction** → beat Gold Standard time
- **Win** → top the cohort race (counts toward [[racesims-the-ladder|Ladder]] Step 1 "win")

---

## What they leave with

- Initiation Licence (lifetime) → Championship-eligible
- A logged baseline lap time on their Driver Card
- Readiness for [[racesims-curriculum-advanced-circuit|Advanced Circuit]] or [[racesims-curriculum-advanced-rally|Advanced Rally]]
