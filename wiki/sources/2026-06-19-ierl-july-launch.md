## Summary
Planning session for the IERL (Indian Esports Racing League) July 2026 soft launch. Covered adding [[racing-point-esports]] (Uday, Hyderabad, 10 rigs) to the July network, revising the prize pool formula from flat ₹2,500/rig to ₹1,00,000 ÷ total rigs, removing a fixed track/car combo in favour of monthly league selection, and drafting the partner WhatsApp message communicating the sim-to-reality vision. Updated the IERL July deck (`ierl-july-2026.html`) across all slides.

## Key facts
- July 2026 network: 7 centres, 6 cities, 27 rigs total
- Prize pool formula: ₹1,00,000 ÷ total rigs = per-rig fee. July: ₹3,704/rig
- Pool is always ₹1,00,000; per-rig cost drops as network grows — strong incentive for partners to recruit
- August preview: Chennai (8 rigs) joins → 35 rigs → ₹2,857/rig, pool unchanged at ₹1L
- Track & car combo chosen monthly by the league (Uday decides); announced before round start
- Platform: Assetto Corsa. Two classes: Amateur (ABS+TC allowed) and Pro (ABS allowed, TC disabled)
- Prizes: Amazon Gift Vouchers. Per class: 1st ₹30K / 2nd ₹12.5K / 3rd ₹7.5K
- Local prizes per centre: ₹500/rig. RPE local prize: ₹5,000 (10 rigs)
- IERL eligibility: triple monitor + 12 Newton metre load cell base minimum + Uday's VMS software
- IERL brand: website live soon, social handles secured; social media management slot is open
- [[arka-motorsports]]: 4 rally and race cars committed to IERL sim-to-reality program
- Coimbatore: dedicated test track (lower cost) + Coimbatore circuit (higher cost) for real racing events
- [[buzzing-hornet-motorsports]]: VW Polo Cup car track experiences in Chennai

## People mentioned
- [[thambu]] — IERL founder, gatekeeping entry, motorsport partnerships
- [[uday]] (Racing Point Esports, Hyderabad) — VMS software lead, technical execution of hot lap events

## Decisions
- Prize pool formula changed to ₹1L ÷ rigs (not flat ₹2,500/rig). Decision: Uday's suggestion.
- Track/car combo: removed fixed Mugello + MX-5/Ferrari from deck. Now chosen monthly by Uday.
- Gifts: "Amazon Gift Cards" renamed to "Amazon Gift Vouchers" throughout deck.
- [[racing-point-esports]] (RPE) added to July network at 10 rigs.
- Exclusivity criteria confirmed: hardware spec (triple monitor + 12Nm load cell) + VMS software + vision alignment.
- Sim-to-reality is the ultimate goal: hot lap → championship → winner drives real race car.

## WhatsApp partner message (approved)
The following was approved for the IERL partner WhatsApp group:

> Hey everyone,
>
> Wanted to share where we're headed with this, and what we need help with as a group.
>
> The hot lap event is the starting point, but the bigger idea is to build something that goes all the way from sim to a real race car. The best drivers who come through IERL should eventually have a path to drive an actual car on an actual track. That's what we're working toward.
>
> On the motorsport side, I work with [[arka-motorsports]] and we're putting 4 rally and race cars into this program. There's a dedicated test track in Coimbatore and the Coimbatore circuit for racing events. I've also worked with [[buzzing-hornet-motorsports]], so we can offer track experiences on a Volkswagen Polo Cup car in Chennai. These are the real-world experiences that sit on top of the league.
>
> We'll be using Uday's software for running the hot lap event and eventually the championship. He's come out with a great product that's going to make things very smooth and easy for all of us. Once it's ready, it handles everything — event management, leaderboards, future championships — and takes most of the operational weight off all of us. He's also run hot lap events multiple times, so he knows exactly how to execute on the ground.
>
> The league runs under one brand — Indian Esports Racing League. The website will be live soon and the social handles are secured.
>
> I've supplied rigs to more sim centres than are in this group, but not all of them are part of this. There are hardware, software, and other requirements that need to be met to be part of the league — all of which you already have. This is important because the experience needs to be uniform for every participant and the competition needs to be fair. That will matter a lot more as the stakes get higher.
>
> A few things still need owners. Social media is one open slot. What else can each of you contribute beyond running the event at your centre? Let's hear it.
