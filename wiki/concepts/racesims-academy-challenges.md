# Academy Challenges & Practice Trainers

The **always-on, self-serve layer** between coached [[racesims-academy-curriculum|Academy]] sessions. Repeatable skill drills and pass/fail challenges that a driver can attempt any time they're on a rig — auto-scored by [[racesims-vms-build|VMS]], posted to the leaderboard, and used as the **gates** that unlock the next skill Level.

> The insight (borrowed from how the best online academies retain learners): coaching is rare, practice is constant. Give drivers a scored thing to *beat* between sessions and they keep coming back — and they arrive at the next coached session already sharper.

---

## Practice Trainers (drills)

Short, focused, scored mini-exercises. No coach needed — the score is the feedback.

| Trainer | Skill | How it's scored |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **Brake Precision Trainer** | Threshold braking + braking points | Brake from speed, stop in a shrinking target box without locking — accuracy + repeatability score |
| **Race Start Trainer** | Launch + run to Turn 1 | Reaction time + clean first corner against AI — start score |
| **Consistency Challenge** | Repeatable laps | Run N laps; scored on how tight your lap-time *spread* is (not outright pace) |
| **Neutral-Steer / Limit Drill** | Feeling the grip limit | Hold the car at the limit through a skid-pad corner — % of lap at optimal slip |
| **Line Trace Drill** | The racing line | Overlay your line vs the ideal; scored on deviation |

Each has a **bronze / silver / gold** standard. Gold ≈ instructor pace.

## Challenge Gates ("Ready to test yourself?")

Each [[racesims-academy-curriculum|skill Level]] ends with a **Challenge Gate** — a pass/fail test the driver must clear before the next Level's coached sessions. This is the [[racesims-academy-curriculum|B·D·A·D]] assessment, made self-serve and repeatable.

| Gate | Sits after | Pass condition |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Licence Lap** | Initiation (Level 1) | Beat the benchmark lap → [[racesims-the-ladder|Initiation Licence]] |
| **Consistency Gate** | Level 2 | 5 laps inside a tight delta window |
| **Precision Gate** | Level 3 | Hit apex + braking targets across a full lap |
| **Limit Gate** | Level 4 | Beat the Gold Standard lap → Championship-ready |

Fail a gate, you repeat the drill — that integrity is what makes the certificate worth something.

## How it plugs into the rest

- **VMS** auto-scores every attempt and writes it to the driver's Driver Card. See [[racesims-vms-build]].
- The **always-on layer** of the [[racesims-championship-architecture|Championship]] — the lounge screen shows the live House Combo + trainer leaderboards.
- Feeds **Hot Lap** standings (and the IERL leaderboard) where relevant.
- Drives **retention + repeat visits** between paid coached sessions — the commercial reason it exists.

## Strategic role
- **Engagement engine** — a reason to walk in and drive when you don't have a coached session booked.
- **Honest gating** — progression is earned against a number, not attendance.
- **Content** — gold-standard challenge runs and leaderboard battles are social-media gold.
