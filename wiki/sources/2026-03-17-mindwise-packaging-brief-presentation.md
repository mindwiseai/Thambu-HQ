## 2. 4-Side Messaging Architecture

### **Side 1 — FRONT (Hero)**

**Purpose:** Immediate brand recognition + premium positioning

```
[Mindwise Logo]

Your Unfair Advantage

India's Only Government-Backed Brain Supplement

High-potency brain supplement formulation with 55% Bacopa Monnieri (CDRI-08) extract

60 Capsules

[Vegetarian symbol — green dot]
```

**Design principle:** Apple-like minimalism — "not one extra element."

**Key decisions:**
- Use phrase "brain supplement" (not pharmaceutical)
- "Government-backed" establishes regulatory credibility
- State "60 capsules" upfront for transparency
- Exclude dosage instructions (reserved for outer box)

**Reading hierarchy:**
1. Mindwise (brand)
2. Your Unfair Advantage (tagline — [[your-unfair-advantage]])
3. India's Only Government-Backed Brain Supplement
4. High-potency formulation statement
5. 60 capsules (quantity)

---

### **Side 2 — SCIENCE (Credibility)**

**Purpose:** Reasons to believe + bacoside differentiation

```
Not All Bacopa Is Created Equal

Bacopa's effectiveness relies on bacosides. Clinical gold standard is 50% bacosides; Mindwise delivers a higher, optimized 55%.

This 55% potency ensures:
• Greater consistency
• More concentrated active compounds
• Reliable, long-term cognitive support

Only Mindwise has 40+ years of research. 7 clinical trials. Patented, scientifically standardized Bacopa extract developed by [[csir-cdri|CSIR-CDRI]].

Purity and precision are essential for brain performance.
```

**Key facts extracted:**
- Bacopa effectiveness = bacosides concentration
- Clinical standard = 50% bacosides
- Mindwise formulation = 55% bacosides (optimized, per [[55-percent-bacosides]])
- [[cdri-08-pharmacology|CDRI-08 pharmacology]] backed by:
  - 40+ years of research
  - 7+ clinical trials (e.g., [[stough-2008-90-day-trial]], [[stough-2001-12-week-trial]], [[roodenrys-2002-retention-trial]], [[barbhaiya-2008-elderly-trial]])
  - [[csir-cdri|CSIR-CDRI]] standardization + patenting

**Design principle:** Educational, not marketing fluff. Each statement must be defensible against regulatory scrutiny.

---

### **Side 3 — FORMULATION PURITY**

**Purpose:** Clean formulation claims + regulatory badges

```
[CSIR-CDRI logo — top, per [[csir-cdri-logo-requirement]]]

Clinically Proven CDRI-55 Extract

[Icons: GMP Certified | Allergen-Free | Gluten-Free | Vegan]

Refer to outer packaging for detailed information.

Check the bottom of the jar for batch details.
```

**Key regulatory elements:**
- GMP certified (manufacturing standard)
- Allergen-free (major claim — back with formulation data)
- Gluten-free (major claim — back with formulation data)
- Vegan (vegetarian capsules — HPMC, per [[packaging-content-layout]]; note: NOT cruelty-free due to [[cdri-08-safety-profile|animal testing history]])

**Decisions locked:**
- ✅ Drop "Cruelty free" claim (product underwent toxicity testing on rats, rabbits, rhesus monkeys — see [[cdri-08-safety-profile]])
- ✅ Use "Vegetarian capsules" not "Vegan" (HPMC capsules are vegetarian; vegan claim requires separate certification)
- 🔁 CSIR-CDRI logo artwork still being finalized ([[csir-cdri-logo-requirement]])
- 🔁 QR code destination undecided: website / Amazon / Linktree

---

### **Side 4 — BENEFITS**

**Purpose:** Consumer-facing cognitive benefits (aspirational)

```
[4-quadrant benefit box — design reference: existing Mindwise bottle]

• Sharper Focus & Memory
• Clarity on Demand
• Optimum Potency
• Sustained Performance
```

**Rationale:** Benefits language being refined from [[keenmind-agreement-expiry|KeenMind Australia pamphlets]] (Thambu shared reference materials March 23).

**Design principle:** Avoid clinical jargon. Position as daily cognitive edge, not medical treatment.

## 3. Outer Packaging (Cardboard Box)

### Physical dimensions

| Attribute | Rectangle | Cylinder |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Dimensions** | 8 cm × 4.5 cm | 9.6 cm × 4.8 cm |
| **Shape** | Rectangle (even for cylinder bottles) | Rectangle (even for cylinder bottles) |
| **Material** | Cardboard |

### Content layout

| Face | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Front** | Same as bottle (hero messaging — Mindwise + "Your Unfair Advantage" + "India's Only Government-Backed Brain Supplement" + 60 capsules) |
| **Side 1** | Nutritional information, direction of use, dosage |
| **Side 2** | Available for additional content (pamphlet-style, reference KeenMind Australia) |
| **Back** | Same as bottle Side 2 (Science messaging — "Not All Bacopa Is Created Equal...") |

### Design principle

**All regulatory clutter on the box, keep the bottle premium-clean.**

This aligns with [[packaging-content-layout|April 8 decision]]: the bottle is the hero visual asset; the box handles mandatory regulatory burden.

⚠️ **Status uncertain:** [[outer-cardboard-box-decision|The outer box may be dropped]] at launch (cost/logistics). If dropped, bottle label will require full re-layout to absorb nutritional + regulatory content.

## 5. Two-bottle SKU strategy

### D2C (60-capsule bottle)

- **Target:** Performance-oriented high-earner
- **Price point:** ~₹850-900 (per [[pricing-strategy]])
- **Supply duration:** 1 month (at 2 caps/day)
- **Retention strategy:** **Encourage 2-3 bottle minimum** to span [[8-12-week-onset-claim|the 8-12 week onset window]]
- **Marketing implication:** [[brain-health-community-vision|Community-first positioning]] must educate buyers upfront — otherwise they quit after 1 bottle

### B2B (30-capsule blister packs)

- **Format:** Blister packs (not bottles)
- **Two dimensions:**
  - `Package(70_10_120).pdf` — smaller blister option
  - `Package(115_75_125).pdf` — larger blister option
- **Target:** Retail / pharmacy distribution, distributor channel
- **Rationale:** Lower entry point for wholesale customers
