# Drive · Train · Compete — RaceSims Positioning Framework

The three-word framework that organizes every product, every marketing message, every customer journey at any [[racesims-chennai-flagship|RaceSims Experience Centre]]. Locked May 17 in [[2026-05-17-racesims-chennai-flagship-planning|the flagship planning session]] to solve the "too many products, no clear hierarchy" problem.

## The framework

**Every product fits under one of three words. In that order. That is the customer journey.**

```
DRIVE ──────────────► TRAIN ──────────────► COMPETE
  │                      │                      │
You try it          You get serious         You race others
  │                      │                      │
Walk-in              Race Academy           Hot Lap Challenge
Member Pass          Rally School           RS League
                     Tuner Lab              ──────────────►
                                            Sim-to-Reality
                                            (Polo Cup / ARKA)
```

## DRIVE — come in and drive

For walk-ins, casual users, corporate groups. No commitment, no progression required.

| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| Drop In | ₹700/hr weekday · ₹900/hr weekend |
| Member Pass 8 | ₹5,000/month (8 hours) |
| Member Pass 16 | ₹9,000/month (16 hours) |
| Group Experience | ₹8,000/hr (all 6 seats, 2-hr minimum) |

Four products. Easy menu. Anyone walks in and there's a clear answer to "how do I try this?"

## TRAIN — structured programmes with a coach

For drivers who want to get measurably faster. Three schools, each with a clear customer:

| Programme | Price | What it is |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Initiation Programme | ₹8,000 | 4 sessions, the entry point to [[racesims-the-ladder|the Ladder]] — earn your Initiation Licence |
| Race Academy | ₹14,000 | 8 coached sessions, circuit racing fundamentals |
| Rally School | ₹16,000 | 10 coached sessions, stage craft — endorsed by [[arka-motorsports|ARKA]] |
| Tuner Lab | ₹28,000/person | 4-day intensive, vehicle dynamics + setup theory, corporate cohort minimum 4 |

Three schools cover three distinct customer segments (aspiring circuit racer, rally driver, engineer/mechanic). Nothing overlaps.

## COMPETE — race against other people

For drivers who want their lap times to matter. See [[racesims-championship-architecture]] for the full 4-layer structure.

| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| [[racesims-championship-architecture|Championship Pass]] — Circuit | ₹4,500/year |
| Championship Pass — Rally | ₹4,500/year |
| Hot Lap weekly | Included in Pass / drop-in |
| Quarterly Shootout entry | Earned by qualifying |
| Grand Final entry | Earned by Top-5-in-2-of-3-quarters or Top-10 annual points |

**Sim-to-Reality Pathways** are not products you buy — they are destinations you earn via [[racesims-the-ladder|the Ladder]]. Real motorsport seats (Polo Cup Follow Car, ARKA Rally Drive) await the year's Annual Champion.

## The positioning statement (locked)

> **RaceSims — Drive it. Train for it. Race for real.**

Sub-line for website / front door:

> *India's first professional sim racing facility. Open to all. Built for drivers.*

## Why this framework works

Five tests every positioning framework must pass:

1. **Single-glance comprehension** — three words, anyone gets it in 5 seconds
2. **No product orphans** — every existing or future product fits cleanly
3. **Built-in customer progression** — the framework itself implies the customer journey
4. **Marketing simplicity** — one month run DRIVE ads (awareness), next month TRAIN (conversion), next month COMPETE (community). Rotate. Never confuse the message.
5. **Sales conversation script** — staff can use the framework verbatim: *"Three ways to be part of RaceSims — drive, train, or compete. Which sounds like you?"*

## How it appears in each customer channel

**Website** — three buttons on homepage: DRIVE, TRAIN, COMPETE. That is the navigation.

**Walk-in customer** — "Have you driven here before? Let me show you the three ways to be part of RaceSims."

**Corporate client** — goes straight to DRIVE / Group Experience.

**Motorsport parent with a 16-year-old** — starts DRIVE, moves to TRAIN (Race Academy), eyes COMPETE.

**Existing rally driver** — Rally School → ARKA Pathway via Ladder.

**Marketing campaigns** — one focus per month. Never mix the three message types in one ad.

## What this replaces

The framework solved a real problem in earlier conversations: there were too many disconnected products (walk-in, memberships, 4 training programmes, 2 sim-to-reality programmes, hot lap events, championship leagues, Polo Cup partnership, ARKA partnership) with no clear hierarchy. Drive · Train · Compete is the spine that makes all of them legible.
