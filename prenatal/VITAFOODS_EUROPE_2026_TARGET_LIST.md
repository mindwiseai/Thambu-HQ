## Pre-trip prep — DO THESE BEFORE YOU GO

1. **Print 50 business cards** with: Aishwarya Chandrasekaran, Founder, The Edit, theedit.in (or whichever name you lock by then), phone, email
2. **Have a 30-second pitch ready** — the manifesto: *"I'm building India's answer to a wellness market that hasn't kept up with how modern Indian couples actually live. Looking for premium branded ingredients for our preconception, prenatal, and postnatal SKUs."*
3. **Print a one-pager of your formulation spec** — list of ingredients you want sourced, MOQs you can commit to, target launch date. Ingredient suppliers will ask.
4. **Decide who's going** — you alone, or you + Thambu? Two people can cover 2x the booths.
5. **Pre-book meetings** — Vitafoods has a meeting concierge. Book the must-meets ahead, especially Balchem, dsm-firmenich, Gnosis (Quatrefolic), and Ixoreal (KSM-66).
6. **Bring a portable scanner / Notion mobile** — booth conversations move fast. Capture every business card + notes immediately.
7. **Plan flights to land May 4 evening** — register Day 1 (May 5) at 9am sharp. The first 2 hours have the lowest crowd.
