# Route 1: Peak Mind & Stoic Mind

**Status:** Explored in March 23 visual identity meeting; **not locked** (Route 3 selected instead)  
**Source:** [[2026-03-23-mindwise-visual-identity-meeting]]

Both expressions share a core philosophy — mastery and mental clarity — but diverge in visual intensity.

---

## Route 1A: The Peak Mind (Expansion)

### Concept
For people with a **growth mindset** where life is not about maintaining balance but **reaching the next level**. Students chasing mastery, professionals seeking flow, individuals constantly pushing themselves toward better performance.

The identity visualizes that ambition through a **mountain peak or pyramid** treatment.

### Design

**Typography:** Structured, powerful, confident — a fitting foundation for a brand around pushing mind beyond distraction.

**Form:** 
- Expands **outward on both sides** → suggests elevation, progress, achievement through sheer expansion
- Rises upward → quiet metaphor for pursuit of peak cognitive performance
- **Downward extension** also added → duality of perspective (peak + path to get there)
- Mixed case treatment (uppercase base + lowercase) → 3D appeal, ascent reinforced

**Insignia:** M + W **combined separately** (not overlapped)

**Color:** Neon green + black (same as Route 3)

### Reaction

**Positive:**
- "Goal-driven, aspirational, go-getter spirit"
- "Takes liberty to misbehave, takes space in world of well-behaved pharma brands"
- "Flows fluently together"

**Concern:**
- **Too bold / intimidating** — one participant (Tanya): "anything coming across bold, I take a step back"
- Overstates assertiveness vs. [[positioning-shift]] intent (which is performance-driven, not domineering)

---

## Route 1B: The Stoic Mind (Restraint)

### Concept
For decades, the brain has been misunderstood as something that must simply **focus harder**. But true strength **rarely needs to shout**. In a world saturated with noise and visual overload, **confidence reveals itself through restraint**.

Real performance is not about flash. It's about **clarity, control, composure** — qualities that come from consistency, not hype.

### Design

**Typography:** Clean, grounded, minimal — allows weight and structure of letterforms to carry identity **without excessive intervention**.

**Form:** **No expansion effect** (vs 1A)
- Still bold and confident — "can't take this brand for granted"
- But **calm, controlled, quietly powerful** personality
- Comes from consistency rather than hype

**Insignia:** M + W **joined together** in overlapping **zigzag pattern** (vs 1A separate treatment)
- Represents the cohesive thinking a peak-performing brain is capable of
- Suggests interconnection, neural networks (subtle tie to Route 2 thinking)

**Color:** Neon green + black (but with subtle color differentiation across variants)

### Reaction

**Positive:**
- "Standing tall and definitely"
- "Flows fluently together"
- "Quietly standing out" ← key phrase (vs boldly standing out, 1A)
- More nuanced, less intimidating
- Still has presence without aggression
- Better balance of "strength + wisdom"

**Internal assessment:**
- Less polarizing than 1A
- Better bridges aggressive (1A) ↔ smart (Route 3) spectrum
- But Route 3 ultimately won because it felt "smarter" than "restraint"

---

## Why Route 1 Was Not Locked

From meeting consensus (2026-03-23):

> "All of us universally had this was the third one... the others help you understand the journey, but 3 is bang out there..."

- **1A** felt too bold — risked brand appearing domineering rather than performance-enabling
- **1B** better than 1A (restraint > expansion) but still didn't have the **"smart"** quality that Route 3 conveyed
- **Route 3** had unanimous emotional pull — instantly intriguing, no second-guessing

The team kept Routes 1A & 1B in deck as **journey context** — showing the evolution of thinking that led to Route 3.

---

## Design Rationale

### Route 1's core strength
Both expressions anchor to **mastery and upward performance** — legitimate brand narrative. For high-performers and growth-mindset buyers, the peak/mountain metaphor resonates.

### Why it lost to Route 3
- Less distinctive insignia treatment (M+W combination is visible in other routes too)
- No single "aha" moment (Route 3's M→brain transformation is more memorable)
- More conventional in supplement category (peak/mountain metaphor used elsewhere)
