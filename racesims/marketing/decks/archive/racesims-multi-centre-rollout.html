02 · What is RaceSims?

India's integrated motorsport development platform.

Indian motorsport is in a moment most of us have waited a long time for. F1 viewership in India crossed 50 million in the last season, growing 25%+ year on year. The INRC delivers close racing season after season. ARKA Motorsports has spent four decades building championship pedigree. The audience is there. The talent is there. What's been missing is a way in.

Padel, cricket nets, climbing gyms, tennis clubs — every other sport has a casual on-ramp. Motorsport hasn't. The cost of entry has kept it out of reach. And for those who want to take it further into real racing, the ladder up has been narrow and gated for too long.

RaceSims is built for both. A place where anyone can try motorsport for the price of a movie ticket — and where those serious about it can find a real path to the racing grid through partnerships with ARKA and Polo Cup.

01
The equipment
Direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, load-cell pedals — the same technology behind F1 driver simulators — has finally become affordable enough to deploy commercially in India.
02
The pedigree
Founded by a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports (130+ national championship titles). Every curriculum, every coaching session, every centre standard is shaped by motorsport people. The Ladder pathway to real-world seats is real.
03
The integration
Official distributor and service partner in India for Conspit and VNM — favourable hardware economics on every centre. But the real edge isn't the kit — it's that we operate the full development stack under one brand: centres + hardware + championship + curriculum + sim-to-real pathway. Everyone else plays one part. We play all of them.
