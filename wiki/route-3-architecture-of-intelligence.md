# Route 3: Architecture of Intelligence

**Status:** LOCKED as primary visual identity direction (March 23, 2026)  
**Color:** Neon green + black  
**Font:** Helvetica Neue (Mind bold, wise light)

---

## Concept

The brain is the **foundation of everything** we build in life — our ambitions, ideas, progress. Without a healthy brain, none of it stands. Route 3 frames the brain as an **architectural structure**: something strong, foundational, essential.

This identity is the locked visual direction for [[your-unfair-advantage]] and all downstream brand collateral.

---

## Design Details

### Insignia
The letter **M transforms into a brain silhouette** — not a literal drawing, but a **geometric, modern interpretation**.

- Hints at the letter M while resembling a **protective roof-like form**
- Reveals brain silhouette on closer inspection → **layered metaphor**
- Works as standalone icon (favicon, favicon, merch, emblem)
- Philosophy: *Protects and gets the best out of your mind*

### Typography
**Helvetica Neue** — brings clarity and timelessness.

- **"Mind"** set in **bold** (weight, strength, credibility)
- **"wise"** set in **lighter weight** (fluidity of wisdom, subtlety)
- Creates visual balance: strength ↔ subtlety
- **Compound form** "mind-wise" (not "mind wise") → better legibility + reads as single entity

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- **Neon green** — energy, performance, mental agility, clarity (Mindwise brand promise)
- **Black** — contrast, power, grounding

**Application:**
- Logo: neon on black (or vice versa for inverted applications)
- On bottle: neon logo on semi-transparent/matte finish bottle (shows green capsules)
- Fallback: if neon bottle not feasible, black bottle with neon label

---

## Design Rationale

### Why this route won

From the meeting (2026-03-23):

> "All of us universally had this was the third one... the others help you understand the journey, but 3 is bang out there, like it has nothing to do with..."

Unanimous internal consensus. Not the boldest, but the **smartest**. Doesn't intimidate; it **intrigues**.

- **Legible:** Instantly clear in-store and on-screen
- **Memorable:** Geometric brain + bold-light typography creates distinct visual signature
- **Symbolic:** Anchors the entire value prop (architecture = foundation = your brain as the foundation)
- **Sophisticated:** Avoids cliché of literal brain drawings (common in supplement category)
- **Adaptable:** Works as logo, insignia, icon, pattern

### How geometric brain avoids category cliché

The category (pharma, supplements, brain health) has beaten the brain silhouette to death — literal squiggles, hemispheres, generic renderings. This route transforms the **M itself into a brain shape**, making the brain a **functional letterform** rather than an ornament. Genius in constraint.

## Packaging Integration

### Bottle Strategy
See [[semi-transparent-bottle]] for full packaging lock.

- **Bottle material:** Semi-transparent with matte finish
- **Why:** Shows neon-green capsules inside, makes neon logo **pop**
- **Logo placement:** Neon logo (on bottle or label)
- **Base bottle colors explored:** white, black, blue, clear, frosted
- **Ideal:** neon-green bottle sleeve (like comparison brand) — but volume constraints force labels
- **Fallback:** black bottle + neon label (easier to source, approved as backup)
