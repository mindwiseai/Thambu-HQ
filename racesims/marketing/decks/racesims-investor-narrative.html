What is RaceSims?

Indian motorsport is in a moment most of us have waited a long time for. F1 viewership is growing every year, the INRC is delivering season after season of close racing, and teams like ARKA Motorsports have spent four decades building championship pedigree that quietly rivals anything in Asia. The audience is there. The talent is there.

What's been missing is two things at once.

For the millions of people who'd love to try motorsport — to feel the wheel, the brakes, a hot lap — there's been no easy way in. Padel courts, cricket nets, climbing gyms, tennis clubs have made trying a sport feel casual and affordable. Motorsport hasn't had that. The cost of entry has kept it out of reach.

And for those who want to take it further — to develop the craft and one day race for real — the ladder up has been narrow, expensive, and gated for too long.

RaceSims is built for both. A place where anyone can try motorsport for the price of a movie ticket, and where those serious about it can find a real path to the racing grid.

We are India's integrated motorsport development platform — operating premium centres, distributing the best sim racing hardware, hosting national championships, and through partnerships with ARKA and Polo Cup, putting top graduates into real-world seats. We're not replacing the motorsport ecosystem. We're adding a new on-ramp to it — one wide enough for casual curiosity, steep enough for serious ambition.

Three things make this possible now

01 · The equipment
Direct-drive wheelbases, motion platforms, and load-cell pedals — the same technology behind F1 driver simulators — has finally become affordable enough to deploy commercially in India.
02 · The pedigree
As a working race engineer at ARKA Motorsports (130+ national championship titles since 1987), every curriculum, every coaching session, every centre standard is shaped by people who have stood on real race grids. RaceSims is sim racing built by motorsport people. That's also what makes our pathway real — top Motorsport Academy graduates earn real-world seat opportunities through our partnerships with ARKA and the Polo Cup ecosystem.
03 · The integration
RaceSims is a direct distributor in India for both Conspit and VNM — the world's leading premium sim racing brands. Every centre we build is at trade pricing — a ₹65L spec for ₹39L of capital. But the deeper edge isn't the hardware cost — it's that we operate the full development stack under one brand: centres + hardware + championship + curriculum + sim-to-real pathway. Everyone else in this market plays one part of that. We play all of them.

RaceSims is three businesses under one brand. We operate premium sim racing centres — the flagship opens in Chennai in 2026, with a 43-centre national rollout mapped over five years. We distribute Conspit and VNM hardware nationally. And we organise the National Sim Racing Championship — launching in 2027, with broadcast, sponsors, and a real seat at the end for the season's best.

Inside every centre, Open Sessions (drop-in, ₹500-1,000/hr, walk-in welcome) sit alongside The Motorsport Academy — the structured curriculum from Initiation through Race Academy, Rally School, and Pro Track for those who want a serious path. The Ladder takes top Academy graduates the rest of the way — through ARKA Rally Drive and Polo Cup Follow Car — to real-world racing seats.

Where I see RaceSims in three years

There's a particular kind of dream that nearly every Indian teenager has held at some point — racing driver. But more broadly, there are millions of Indians who'd love to try motorsport — to feel a real wheel, brake into a corner, drive a hot lap — and have never had an affordable way in. Most other sports have a casual on-ramp. Motorsport hasn't.

By May 2029, RaceSims will be two things, equally important.

The most accessible motorsport experience in India. A place where a teenager, a corporate team, a parent on a Sunday afternoon, or someone curious after watching Drive to Survive can walk in for the first time and have a real motorsport experience for the price of a movie ticket. The cricket-net, padel-court, climbing-gym equivalent for motorsport — finally.

The platform where Indian motorsport development starts. For those who want to take it further, the runway is here — the Motorsport Academy, the RaceSims Championship, and a path to real-world seats via ARKA Rally Drive and Polo Cup.

The moats that hold it together

The pedigree moat — ARKA exclusivity papered, first Academy graduates in ARKA cars, real seats earned by real drivers. Years to build, almost impossible to replicate.

The integration moat — the only brand in India running centres + championship + curriculum + sim-to-seat pathway under one roof. Others compete with one slice. We hold the whole.

The cost moat — direct Conspit and VNM distribution. Every centre built at trade pricing. The national network funds itself off operating cash from Year 3 onward.

The network moat — 17 centres feeding into one another's leaderboards, championship rounds, and community. The brand becomes a flywheel, not a venue chain.

The brand moat — "Motorsport Academy graduate" becomes a recognised credential inside the Indian motorsport world. That language takes years to earn and is permanent once it lands.

What winning looks like at Year 3

It isn't a slide in a deck. It's two things at once.
