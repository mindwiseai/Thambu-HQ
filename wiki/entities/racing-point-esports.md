## Overview
Racing Point Esports (RPE) is a sim racing centre based in Hyderabad, India. Run by [[uday]]. Joined the [[ierl]] network in July 2026 as the largest single contributor by pod count.

**Location:** Hyderabad
**Owner/Operator:** [[uday]]
**Pods:** 8 (confirmed 2026-07-07)

## IERL role
- 8 pods in the July 2026 network → contributes ₹18,400 to the pool (8 × ₹2,300)
- No local prizes in the July round
- In settlement, RPE pays Pro 1st (₹17,500) + part of Pro 2nd (₹900)
- [[uday]] also leads technical execution of the hot lap event and VMS software
- [[uday]] decides track and car selection each month for the IERL round
