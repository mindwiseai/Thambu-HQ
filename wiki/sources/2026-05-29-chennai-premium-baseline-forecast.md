## Summary
Working session with [[thambu]] rebuilding the [[racesims-chennai-flagship|Chennai]] business case from scratch as a **conservative, lights-on baseline** — sim sessions only, run under [[racesims-company|RaceSims Solutions Pvt Ltd]]. Iterated through several input corrections (rent ₹1,55,250; equipment at distributor cost; GST-inclusive pricing; 3 staff; real maintenance reserve; 5 race + 1 motion rig). Crucially anchored occupancy to **real dealer data from [[uday-hyderabad|Uday]]** instead of the investor deck's industry benchmarks, which materially lowered the forecast. Output: live Excel model + [[racesims-chennai-premium-baseline]] concept page. Also surfaced Thambu's genuine nervousness about the overhead jump (from ~₹90k/mo known burn to ₹3.17L) and worked through the risk framing.

## Key facts
- Config locked: **5 race/rally + 1 three-DOF motion rig**, equipment ₹39,09,400 distributor cost.
- Tiered premium pricing: race ₹750/₹1,250, motion ₹1,000/₹1,600; **fleet blended ₹1,225/hr net of GST**.
- Monthly OpEx ₹3,17,000 (lean 2-staff option ₹2,92,000).
- Net cash deployed ₹57.6L; sunk ₹48.3L; break-even **13.8% occupancy**.
- Year 1 (brand-lands): ₹49.8L net rev, **₹11.7L EBITDA**, ₹3.5L max drawdown, profitable month 5.
- Year-1 income tax ≈ ₹4k via depreciation shield, even **without** [[startup-tax-exemption|80-IAC exemption]] (not claimed this year).
- Payback **~19-22 months** at 25% steady — corrected down from an earlier erroneous "~8-10 months".

## Decisions
- Go **premium pricing** lane (vs matching Uday's ₹800/hr volume play).
- Exclude academy/café/Ladder from the baseline — prove drop-in first, stack later.
- Open lean (recommendation): 2 staff, add 3rd when weekends force it.
- Hold a ~6-month (~₹19L) runway buffer separate from capital; ring-fence personal house rent.
