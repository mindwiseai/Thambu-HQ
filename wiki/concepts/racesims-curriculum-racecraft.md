# Curriculum — Racecraft

**The parallel pillar to car handling.** [[racesims-curriculum-advanced-circuit|Advanced]] teaches you to beat the clock; Racecraft teaches you to beat *other people*. Delivered through **[[racesims-championship-architecture|Championship]] participation + coached masterclasses** for licensed drivers — this is the **COMPETE** side of [[racesims-drive-train-compete|Drive · Train · Compete]].

> The skill that wins Live Races (and so unlocks [[racesims-the-ladder|The Ladder]] Step 2) is not lap time — it's racecraft. A fast qualifier who can't race wheel-to-wheel never reaches the real seat.

Four modules, taught **mistake-first**.

---

## 1 · Racecraft Foundations
- **The rules of racing** — one move to defend, leave a car's width, the racing-room convention. Clean racing is *faster* racing.
- **The skills that matter** — awareness, patience, track position, knowing when *not* to attack.
- **Simple habits that beat most people** — be where they aren't, win the exit, never crash yourself out of a points finish.

## 2 · Battle Dynamics
*The mechanics of wheel-to-wheel.*
- **Positioning & braking in traffic** — braking later/earlier than you would alone; defending the inside line.
- **Chaining corners under pressure** — sacrificing a corner to set up the pass two corners later.
- **The switchback / cutback** — give up the entry, win the exit, pass on the way out (see [[racesims-academy-curriculum|glossary]]).
- **The divebomb** — the late, deep inside lunge: when it's on, when it's just a crash.
- **The squeeze** — using track position to make an opponent lift.
- **Multi-car fights** — the three-wide problem; protecting against the car you *can't* see.
- **Drafting, slipstream & dirty air** — the tow on the straight, the grip you lose following closely.

## 3 · Racing Strategies
- **Race starts & the run to Turn 1** — the highest-value, highest-risk 10 seconds of any race.
- **Tyre & fuel over a race** — managing a stint to be fastest *at the end*; lift-and-coast where it pays.
- **Qualifying vs race mindset** — the one-lap hero vs the points-finisher.

## 4 · Psychology of Racing
*The dimension most academies skip.*
- **Knowledge is power** — preparation removes fear; the prepared driver is the calm one.
- **Assertiveness, not aggression** — claim the space you're entitled to without binning it.
- **Commitment** — the half-committed move is the dangerous one; decide, then go.
- **Composure** — racing the long game, recovering from a mistake instead of compounding it.
