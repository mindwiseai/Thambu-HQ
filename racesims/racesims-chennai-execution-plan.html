Adding sim subscriptions (~₹9k), single-venue VMS cloud (~₹3–5k), insurance (~₹7.5k), payment gateway (~2% of revenue) and Year-1 marketing (~₹25k) lifts the realistic all-in to ~₹3.6–3.9L/mo. Runway buffer: hold ~₹17L separate (FD/liquid) — covers the worst-case (Brand Lags) ₹7.6L drawdown plus a motion-rig repair contingency, with room to spare. Ring-fence personal house rent from the business.

10KPIs & guardrails

Track weekly (Mon, 5 min): rig-hours sold, motion-rig utilisation, weekly revenue, new Driver Cards, repeat-customer %. Month-4 hard checkpoint: occupancy must be >10% and trending up, cumulative drawdown <₹10L, ≥2 corporate bookings done, motion rig >30% booked. If any two fail → trigger mitigation. Layer academy at >15% sustained; café at >20%; 2nd floor when weekday peak >60% for 3 months.
